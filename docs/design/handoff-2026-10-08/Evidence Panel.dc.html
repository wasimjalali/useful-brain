Version2.0 Effective1 Feb 2026 Readable byEveryone OwnerHR

Overview and Eligibility

Northwind Systems offers paid parental leave to all regular employees, regardless of gender, office location, or how a child joins the family. Eligibility requires six months of continuous service before the leave starts. The policy covers birth parents, non-birth parents, adoptive parents, and parents via surrogacy. Parental leave is an entitlement on top of the annual leave in the Leave and Time Off Policy; it does not reduce your 20/25/18 annual days, your sick days, or your sabbatical eligibility. This policy is administered by the HR team and was updated on February 1, 2026.

Paid Leave Duration

1Every eligible parent receives sixteen weeks of fully paid parental leave per child. The sixteen weeks are paid at 100% of base salary and are available to each parent individually: if both parents work at Northwind, both may take the full sixteen weeks. Leave can be taken all at once or in up to two blocks, but each block must be at least two weeks long. The sixteen weeks are per child, not per year, so twins do not double the entitlement.

Timing and Notice

Parental leave must start within twelve months of the child's birth, adoption placement, or surrogacy arrangement completion. You must give your manager at least four weeks of notice before the leave start date, or as much notice as practicable for early arrivals.
