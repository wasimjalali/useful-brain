1Parental Leave PolicyPaid Leave Duration

Every eligible parent receives sixteen weeks of fully paid parental leave per child. The sixteen weeks are paid at 100% of base salary and are available to each parent individually: if both parents work at Northwind, both may take the full sixteen weeks.

2Parental Leave PolicyOverview and Eligibility

Northwind Systems offers paid parental leave to all regular employees, regardless of gender, office location, or how a child joins the family. Eligibility requires six months of continuous service before the leave starts.
