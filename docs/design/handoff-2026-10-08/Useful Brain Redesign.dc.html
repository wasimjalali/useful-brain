1 Useful Brain redesign 7 Oct 2026 · 18 artboards from the brief, light first, dark for 01, 02 and 10
SignatureThe graphite highlight. The sentence an answer relies on is marked in ink at 12% behind the text; hovering its citation darkens it to 24%. Nothing else on any screen gets that treatment. ShellFlat 248px rail on the canvas, white stage floating at a 10px gutter with a 24px radius. The evidence panel is a second stage, never an overlay, so the answer and its proof sit side by side. ColourMonochrome. Green, amber and red appear only on real status: system health, ingestion, eval results, errors. MonoGeist Mono only where a value is exact: citations, chunk and generation IDs, scores, tool arguments, latencies.
