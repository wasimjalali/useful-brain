# Office and Facilities Policy

## Our Offices

Northwind Systems has three offices: the headquarters in Austin,
Texas; the London office; and the Singapore office. Each office has
the same facilities basics: badge-controlled access, bookable desks,
meeting rooms, a quiet floor, and a parent room. The offices are
owned by the operations team, and each office has an office manager.
The office hours are 7:00 to 19:00 local time, Monday through
Friday; outside those hours, badge access works for employees who
are on the after-hours list (on-call staff, incident responders,
and approved workers). The Remote Work and Flexible Hours Policy
governs who is in the office and when; this policy governs the
physical space.

## Badges and Access

Every employee receives a badge on their first day, issued by IT
under the Recruiting and Interview Policy's onboarding handover.
Badges are personal: sharing a badge is a security violation under
the Security and Vulnerability Policy. Badges open the office
during office hours and, for after-hours list members, outside
hours. Badge access levels: the standard level opens the employee's
own office; the all-offices level (for travelers and teams that
work across offices) is granted by the office manager on request
and is reviewed annually. Visitors do not get badges: they are
signed in by their host, wear a visitor badge, and are escorted in
non-public areas. Lost badges are reported to facilities and
replaced within 2 business days; a lost badge is deactivated
immediately.

## Desk Booking and Meeting Rooms

Desks are bookable through the facilities portal, and hot-desking
applies in all three offices (no permanent desks except for
facilities-adjacent roles). The Remote Work and Flexible Hours
Policy sets the shared office day (Wednesday) and the all-hands day
(first Thursday of each month), when desk demand is highest; desks
can be booked up to 2 weeks ahead. Meeting rooms are bookable up to
30 days ahead, and rooms held for more than 15 minutes past the
start time are released. The parent rooms are bookable by anyone
who needs them, with priority for nursing parents, and the quiet
floor is phone-free. Visitors from other Northwind offices get a
desk for up to 5 consecutive days without approval, per the Remote
Work policy.

## Visitor and Guest Policy

Visitors (candidates, customers, partners, vendors) are signed in
at reception by their host, who confirms the visit in the visitor
system; the visitor wears a visitor badge and is escorted in
non-public areas. Customer visits are coordinated by the CSM or
account manager, and partner visits by the partner manager (see
the Partner Program Policy). Candidates follow the Recruiting and
Interview Policy's scheduling rules. Visitors are covered by the
office safety rules, and a visitor who needs network access gets a
guest network account valid for the visit duration, issued by IT.
Visitors are never left unescorted in areas with customer data
visible, and the host is responsible for the visitor's compliance
with the IP and Confidentiality Policy.

## Office Safety and Security

Each office has a safety warden team, a first aid kit, and an
evacuation plan, and fire drills run quarterly per office. The
safety wardens are trained by the office manager, and the drill
schedule is posted on the facilities portal. Suspicious activity
is reported to the office manager and to security at
security@northwind.example; a security incident in an office is
handled under the Incident Escalation Matrix (SEV-2 or above if
customer data is involved). The Business Continuity Policy covers
what happens when an office cannot be used (for example, a
building issue): the office manager declares the office closed,
employees work remotely, and the business continuity team runs the
response. Mail and packages are handled by reception, and personal
packages are limited to the size that fits in the mail room.

## Parent Rooms and Accessibility

Every office has a parent room (a lockable, private room for
nursing and pumping) and accessible facilities. Parent rooms are
bookable in the facilities portal, and priority goes to nursing
parents; the rooms are cleaned daily and are not used for storage.
Accessibility: all offices are wheelchair accessible, meeting
rooms are bookable with accessibility notes, and employees who need
an accommodation request it through HR (the CHRO's office), not
through facilities. The parent room list is referenced by the
Parental Leave Policy for returning parents. Facilities requests
(repairs, supplies, room issues) are filed in the facilities
portal and answered within 2 business days; urgent issues (no
heat, water damage) are called in to the office manager.

## Facilities Budget and Records

Office costs are planned in the Budget Planning and Forecasting
cycle: each office has a facilities budget line, and capital items
(furniture, equipment) are approved under the Vendor and
Procurement Policy's thresholds. Facilities records (access logs,
visitor logs, maintenance records) are retained for three years
under the Data Retention Policy's operational records, and the
access logs are security records under the System Log Retention
Policy (7-year tier). The quarterly facilities report (occupancy,
room utilization, spend) goes to the COO and feeds the office
planning for the following year.
