# Equipment Return Policy

## Purpose

This policy covers the return of Northwind Systems equipment: laptops,
phones, tablets, badges, monitors, and loaned peripherals. It applies
when an employee leaves the company, transfers roles, or returns
loaner equipment, and it is the operations counterpart to the
Termination and Offboarding Policy's equipment step. It is owned by
operations and administered by IT. Returning equipment is not the
same as returning a product to a vendor, and it is not a refund:
the Refund Policy covers money back for software subscriptions, with
its own windows and approvers. Equipment questions go to
it@northwind.example with the asset code (AST-####).

## Return Windows

Equipment must be returned within 30 calendar days of the event that
triggers the return: the last working day for departures, the role
change date for transfers that change equipment, and the loan end
date for loaner equipment. The 30-day window is measured from the
trigger date, not from the reminder. IT sends a return reminder at
day 7 and day 21 of the window. Equipment returned after the window
is a late return, and the return is logged as late regardless of
condition. Loaner equipment (event laptops, demo units) has a
tighter window: 5 business days from the loan end date, because
loaners are shared. Departing employees who do not return equipment
within the window have the equipment's book value deducted from
final pay under the Termination and Offboarding Policy.

## Return Process

To return equipment: wipe personal data, remove personal accounts
(IT can assist), and deliver the equipment to IT or ship it using
the prepaid label IT provides. IT inspects the equipment within 5
business days of receipt and issues a return confirmation with the
asset code. The return is complete when IT confirms receipt and the
inspection, not when the employee drops it off. For departures, the
manager confirms the handover checklist in the Termination and
Offboarding Policy. Badges are returned on the last working day, not
within the 30-day window: badges control office access (see the
Office and Facilities Policy), and a badge not returned on the last
day is deactivated by facilities the same day.

## Condition and Damage

Equipment is returned in the condition it was issued, normal wear
excepted. Damage beyond normal wear is assessed by IT at
inspection: damage over $500 in repair or replacement cost is
charged to the employee, deducted from final pay for departures or
billed to the employee for transfers. The $500 threshold applies
per incident, not per item. Lost equipment is charged at book
value. Damage assessments can be appealed to the operations manager
within 10 business days, and the appeal is decided within 5
business days. Normal wear (scuffs, worn batteries, keyboard wear)
is not charged. Insurance claims for damaged equipment follow the
Business Continuity Policy's asset protection rules.

## Data Wipe and Security

IT wipes returned equipment within 10 business days of receipt,
using the approved wipe process, and the wipe is logged with the
asset code. Wiping is mandatory before equipment is reissued,
resold, or recycled: no returned device is reissued without a
logged wipe. The wipe covers storage and the firmware-level reset;
IT verifies the wipe on a sample of devices quarterly. Equipment
that cannot be wiped (failed storage) is destroyed by IT, and the
destruction is logged. The security requirements follow the
Security and Vulnerability Policy, and equipment returned from a
security incident (SEV-1 or SEV-2) is quarantined and forensically
preserved before any wipe, per the incident record rules.

## Transfers and Loaners

Role transfers that change equipment follow the same process with a
30-day window from the transfer date: the old equipment is
returned, and the new equipment is issued when the old is confirmed
returned, or on the transfer date for critical roles with manager
approval. Loaner equipment (conference laptops, demo units,
visitor devices) is tracked separately with 5-business-day return
windows, and loaners past 30 days are escalated to the operations
manager. Contractors returning equipment follow this policy's
windows, and the return is confirmed against their final invoice
under the Contractor Expense Policy. Equipment for departing
employees is collected in the exit interview handover (see the
Termination and Offboarding Policy).

## Records and Audits

IT keeps the asset register with the AST-#### codes, the return
dates, the inspection results, and the wipe logs. The register is
audited quarterly by operations: open returns past their window,
unwiped devices, and unreconciled assets. The quarterly audit feeds
the asset protection section of the SOC 2 audit evidence. Asset
records are retained for three years after the asset is retired,
under the Data Retention Policy's operational records. Equipment
that is not returned is reported to the operations manager and, for
departures, to HR, which applies the final pay deduction under the
Termination and Offboarding Policy.

## Relationship to Other Policies

The Refund Policy is the money-back policy for subscriptions and is
not affected by equipment returns: returning a laptop does not
trigger a refund, and a refund does not trigger equipment return.
The Termination and Offboarding Policy covers the departure steps
that this policy's windows implement. The Office and Facilities
Policy covers badges and office access. The Security and
Vulnerability Policy covers the wipe requirements. The Business
Continuity Policy covers asset protection and insurance. The
Contractor Expense Policy covers contractor equipment and the
final invoice charges.
