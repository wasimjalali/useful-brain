# Partner Program Policy

## Purpose

The partner program covers companies that resell, implement, or
integrate Northwind products: resellers, implementation partners,
and technology partners. This policy defines how partners are
onboarded, how partner deals are priced, and how partners are paid.
It is owned by sales and shared with finance, which runs the partner
payments. The program is separate from the Referral Program (which
pays employees a flat $2,000 per customer) and separate from the
employee recruiting referral. Questions go to partners@northwind.example.
Partner agreements are contracts under the Contract Review and
Approval Policy and are signed with codes in the format PT-####.

## Partner Types and Onboarding

There are three partner types. Resellers: sell Northwind products to
end customers under the partner pricing model. Implementation
partners: deliver implementations and training for customers (their
services are billed by the partner, not by Northwind). Technology
partners: build integrations under the API and Integration
Standards. Onboarding requires: a signed partner agreement, a
completed partner application with references, and (for resellers)
a minimum commitment of $50,000 in annual bookings after the first
year. Onboarding is completed within 30 business days of the
application, and partners are reviewed annually: resellers must
maintain the minimum commitment, and technology partners must pass
the integration review.

## Partner Pricing

Reseller deals use partner pricing, which is separate from the
direct discount tiers in the Discount Approval Policy: the partner
price is 80% of list ACV for the first year and 85% for renewals,
set in the partner agreement. The partner invoices the end customer
at list price or higher, and the margin belongs to the partner.
Partner pricing cannot be combined with direct discounts: a deal is
either direct or partner, never both, and this is enforced at
billing. Deal registration: a reseller registers a deal in the
partner portal before the first customer meeting to protect their
margin; unregistered deals are direct deals. The registration window
is 90 days, renewable once.

## Partner Commissions and Payouts

Partners earn a recurring commission of 10% of partner-sourced ACV,
paid quarterly. The commission applies to the invoiced ACV of
registered partner deals: 10% of the annual invoice for the first
year, and 10% of renewal invoices while the customer remains active
and the partner agreement is current. Payouts are made quarterly,
within 30 days of the quarter end, by bank transfer, with a
commission statement showing the invoices and the calculation.
Commissions are paid on paid invoices only, per the Invoicing and
Payment Terms Policy. A partner deal that is refunded under the
Refund Policy claws back the commission on the next payout, under
the same refund windows (14 calendar days for annual plans).

## Implementation and Technology Partners

Implementation partners bill the customer directly for their
services; Northwind does not pay implementation commissions. The
partner's implementation work follows the Customer Onboarding
Checklist's milestones, and the CSM coordinates with the partner.
Technology partners build to the API and Integration Standards, and
their integrations are reviewed by engineering before listing in
the partner directory. Technology partners do not earn commission;
their benefit is the directory listing and the co-marketing terms
in the agreement. A technology partner that resells (rare, and only
with a reseller addendum) moves to reseller rules for the resold
deals.

## Partner Lifecycle

Partner agreements run 12 months with automatic renewal, per the
Contract Review and Approval Policy's standard terms. Partners can
be terminated: for breach (with a 30-day cure period), for falling
below the reseller minimum commitment at the annual review, or for
violating the API standards. Termination ends deal registration
rights, and registered deals in progress are honored for 60 days
after termination. Partner records, agreements, and commission
statements are retained for seven years under the Data Retention
Policy. The partner program is reviewed quarterly by the sales VP
and the CFO: partner-sourced revenue, commission spend, and
registration conversion.

## Relationship to Other Policies

The Discount Approval Policy governs direct deals; partner pricing
is set in this policy and the partner agreement. The Referral
Program pays employees, not partners, and a deal cannot be both a
referral deal and a partner deal. The Sales Commission Plan does
not pay commission to reps on partner-sourced deals. The Contract
Review and Approval Policy governs partner agreements (the partner
agreement is a contract under its thresholds). The API and
Integration Standards govern technology partner integrations. The
Invoicing and Payment Terms Policy governs partner invoices, which
follow Net 30 like direct deals. The Refund Policy governs refunds
on partner deals, which claw back commissions on the next payout.
