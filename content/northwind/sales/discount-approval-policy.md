# Discount Approval Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines who can approve discounts on Northwind product
pricing, and how much. It is owned by sales and is shared with
finance, which enforces the discounts at billing time. The policy
applies to discounts on annual contract value (ACV) for Northwind
Core, Atlas, and Meridian, and to discounts on professional services.
It does not apply to partner pricing, which is set under the Partner
Program Policy, and it does not apply to referral payouts, which are
fixed amounts under the Referral Program. Discounts are set on the
order form at quote time, per the Proposal and Quote Process, and an
unapproved discount is rejected by the billing system.

## Discount Tiers and Approval Authority

Discounts are measured as a percentage off list price, on ACV.
Approval authority: discounts below 10% are approved by the sales
manager (the rep's manager). Discounts from 10% to 20% are approved
by the sales VP. Discounts above 20% require the CFO's approval, and
the CFO reviews them in the weekly pricing review. A discount above
30% is not available at any approval level: the deal is repriced,
restructured, or declined. The tiers apply to the total discount on
the deal, not per line item: stacking a 10% product discount with a
5% services discount is a 15% deal, needing VP approval. Discounts
are expressed as whole numbers, and the pricing system rounds at
quote time.

## Rules of Application

Discounts apply to ACV, which is the annualized value of the
contract, including all seats and modules. Discounts do not apply to:
taxes, one-time setup fees (which have their own fixed pricing),
partner-sourced deals (which follow partner pricing), or add-on
purchases made after the initial contract (which are quoted at list
price unless a new discount approval is attached). A discount
approved for a renewal must be re-approved: the renewal is a new
quote under the Proposal and Quote Process, and prior approvals do
not carry over. Discounts cannot be combined with the partner
program pricing: a deal is either a direct deal with a discount or a
partner deal with partner pricing, never both.

## Discount Justification

Every discount above 10% requires a justification in the pricing
system: the competitive situation, the deal size, or the strategic
reason. Discounts above 20% require a written one-page note from the
sales VP explaining the deal, attached to the quote. The CFO can
reject a discount at any level if the justification is missing, and
the pricing system blocks quotes without the justification field.
Discount approvals are logged with the quote code (QT-####) and are
visible to finance and the sales VP. The monthly pricing review
(second Wednesday) reviews the discount log: total discount spend,
average discount by product, and the discount-to-win-rate ratio.

## Discounts and Compensation

Discounts affect commission under the Sales Commission Plan: commission
is calculated on the invoiced ACV, so a discount reduces the
commission base. There is no commission floor for discounted deals,
and there is no special rate for them. A deal with a discount above
20% is reviewed by the commission administrator when the commission
is calculated, to confirm the discount was approved; an unapproved
discount voids the commission for that deal until the approval is
repaired. Discounts do not change the clawback rules: a refunded or
cancelled deal is clawed back per the commission plan's 90-day rule,
at the discounted value.

## Discounts and Refunds

A discounted deal that is refunded is refunded at the amount paid,
per the Refund Policy: annual plans within 14 calendar days of the
invoice, monthly plans within 7 days. The discount does not reset
the refund window and does not change the refund amount calculation.
A customer who cancels a discounted deal outside the window and
renegotiates is repriced at list, and the new quote needs its own
discount approval. Discount approvals are contract records: they are
retained with the quote and the contract per the Data Retention
Policy (seven years after the contract ends).

## Relationship to Other Policies

The Proposal and Quote Process governs how quotes are built and
issued, and the discount is attached at the quote stage. The Contract
Review and Approval Policy governs the contract thresholds (legal
review at $100,000 ACV and above); a discount approval and a contract
approval are separate chains, and both must be complete before
signature. The Invoicing and Payment Terms Policy enforces the
approved discount at billing, and an invoice with an unapproved
discount is rejected. The Partner Program Policy governs partner
pricing, which is outside this policy. The Sales Commission Plan
uses the discounted ACV as the commission base.
