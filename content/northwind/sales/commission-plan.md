# Sales Commission Plan

## Purpose and Confidentiality

This document defines the commission plan for the Northwind Systems
sales team. It contains the commission rates and is confidential: it
is visible only to sales managers and directors, and the rates are
not shared with individual reps (reps see their own plan summary in
the commission portal, which states their rate without the full
table). The plan is owned by the VP of Sales with finance oversight,
and the CFO approves the plan each year in December for the following
year. The plan year is the calendar year, and the plan applies to
sales roles: account executives, account managers, and sales
directors. Questions go to the commission administrator in finance.

## Commission Rates

Commission is paid as a percentage of invoiced ACV. New business
(deals with customers who have never subscribed, or who were
churned for more than 12 months) pays 8% of ACV. Renewals and
expansion (additional seats or modules on an existing account) pay
4% of ACV. The rate applies to the invoiced amount: a deal signed
with a discount pays commission on the discounted ACV, per the
Discount Approval Policy. There is no commission cap, and there is
no accelerator. Professional services revenue does not earn
commission. Partner-sourced deals are not commissioned to the rep;
they are paid under the Partner Program Policy, and the rep's role
in a partner deal is limited to the registration credit rules of
that policy.

## Commission Calculation

Commission is calculated on invoices paid, not on contracts signed:
the commission for a deal is earned when the customer's first
invoice is paid in full (for annual plans) or when the second
monthly invoice is paid (for monthly plans). The commission base is
the ACV on the invoice, converted at the monthly finance rate for
non-USD invoices (see the Invoicing and Payment Terms Policy).
Commission is calculated by the commission administrator in finance
by the 10th of the month for the prior month's paid invoices.
Disputed or partially paid invoices do not earn commission until
the dispute is resolved under the Invoicing and Payment Terms
Policy.

## Payment Schedule

Commission is paid monthly, with the last paycheck of the month
(the final Friday), covering invoices paid in the prior month.
The payment schedule is monthly, not quarterly, and there is no
holding period beyond the invoice payment requirement. Commission
statements are available in the commission portal by the 15th of
the month, and discrepancies are resolved with the commission
administrator within 10 business days of the statement. Commission
is paid on the normal payroll and is taxed as ordinary income.
Departing salespeople receive earned commission (invoices paid
before their last day) in their final pay under the Termination
and Offboarding Policy; unearned pipeline does not vest.

## Clawbacks

Commission is clawed back when the underlying invoice is refunded or
the account cancels and is refunded, under the Refund Policy's
windows (14 calendar days for annual plans, 7 days for monthly
plans) or the refund exceptions. The clawback window is 90 days
from the commission payment: a refund inside the window claws back
the commission in full from the next commission payment, and a
refund outside the window is absorbed by the company, not the rep.
A rep who leaves with a clawback outstanding has the amount
deducted from final pay under the Termination and Offboarding
Policy. Clawbacks are tracked by the commission administrator, and
the clawback rate is reported quarterly to the CFO.

## Plan Administration

The plan is administered by the commission administrator in finance,
with the sales VP reviewing the monthly calculations. Rate changes
are made only at year-end, by the CFO's approval, and are announced
with the December plan letter; mid-year changes require the CEO's
approval and apply only to new deals. The plan letter is issued to
sales managers each January. Commission disputes follow the
Dispute Resolution and Arbitration Policy's negotiation step, with
the commission administrator's decision appealable to the CFO.
Commission records are retained for seven years under the Data
Retention Policy, and the plan is included in the April and October
SOC 2 audit evidence as a financial control.

## Relationship to Other Policies

The Discount Approval Policy determines the discount, which sets
the commission base. The Refund Policy and the Invoicing and
Payment Terms Policy determine when commission is earned and when
it is clawed back. The Partner Program Policy governs partner
deals, which are not commissioned. The Referral Program pays
employee referral bonuses ($2,000 per customer) which are separate
from commission and are not paid to sales roles for their own
deals. The Termination and Offboarding Policy governs final pay
and clawback deductions. The Territory and Account Assignment
Policy determines which rep owns an account for commission
purposes: commission follows the account owner on the closing
date, and account transfers are handled by that policy.
