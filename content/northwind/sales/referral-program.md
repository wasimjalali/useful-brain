# Referral Program

## Purpose

The referral program pays Northwind employees for introducing new
customers. It is separate from the employee recruiting referral
bonus (which pays $1,000 for a new hire, under the Recruiting and
Interview Policy) and separate from the Partner Program (which pays
partners a recurring commission). This program is for employees
referring customers. It is owned by sales, and questions go to
referrals@northwind.example. The payout is a flat $2,000 per new
customer, and the program is open to all employees except: sales
roles (who are paid commission instead), the CEO, and employees who
work on the account being referred.

## How Referrals Work

An employee refers a prospect by submitting the referral form in the
referral portal before the prospect's first meeting with sales. The
form captures the prospect's company and the referrer's details, and
it creates a referral code in the format RFR-####. A referral is
valid only if submitted before the first sales meeting: referrals
submitted after the prospect is already in the pipeline are
rejected. The referrer does not participate in the sales process;
the account team runs the deal, and the referrer is not the
customer's point of contact. Referrals of current customers, of
companies already in negotiation, or of the referrer's own employer
are not eligible.

## Payout Amount and Timing

The payout is $2,000 per new customer, paid after the customer's
first invoice is paid in full. For annual plans, the first invoice
is the annual invoice, paid under Net 30 terms, so the payout
typically lands 30 to 45 days after the contract start. For monthly
plans, the payout is paid after the second monthly invoice is paid,
to confirm the customer is active. The payout is made in the next
payroll run after the trigger is confirmed by finance, and it is
taxed as ordinary income. If the customer's first invoice is
refunded under the Refund Policy (14 calendar days for annual
plans), the payout is recovered from the next paycheck.

## Eligibility Limits

An employee can refer up to 10 customers per calendar year. There is
no limit on the total payout an employee can earn within that cap.
Referrals from the same company are one customer: a second referral
from a company that already subscribed does not earn a second
payout. Referrals of government entities and public sector accounts
require the legal team's review before the referral is accepted,
because of procurement rules; the review takes up to 10 business
days. Employees who join the sales team during the year lose
referral eligibility on their first day in a sales role, and any
pending referral payouts are paid out under the rules in place when
the referral was submitted.

## Referral Tracking

The referral portal tracks the referral from submission to payout:
submitted, qualified (first meeting held), won, and paid. The
referrer can see the status in the portal, but not the deal value
or the customer's details beyond the company name. Referral records
are kept for seven years under the Data Retention Policy. The
referral program is reported quarterly to the sales VP: referrals
submitted, conversion rate, and total payouts. The program budget
is part of the sales budget in the Budget Planning and Forecasting
cycle, planned at $40,000 per year by default.

## Relationship to Other Policies

The Recruiting and Interview Policy's referral bonus ($1,000 for
hires) is a different program with a different payout, and the two
are not interchangeable. The Partner Program Policy pays partners
10% recurring commission on partner-sourced ACV, which is different
from the flat $2,000 referral payout, and a deal cannot be both a
referral deal and a partner deal. The Discount Approval Policy
applies to the referred customer's deal like any other deal: the
referral payout is not affected by the discount. The Commission
Plan does not pay commission on referred deals to the account rep
beyond the normal deal commission; the referral payout is paid to
the referrer on top. The Refund Policy governs refunds that
trigger payout recovery.
