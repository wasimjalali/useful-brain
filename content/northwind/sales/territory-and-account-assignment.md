# Territory and Account Assignment

## Purpose

This policy defines how accounts and territories are assigned to the
Northwind sales team. It is owned by sales and is shared only within
sales because it contains the territory structure. Assignment
determines who owns a deal, which drives commission under the Sales
Commission Plan: commission follows the account owner on the closing
date. The policy is administered by sales operations, and assignment
disputes are resolved by the sales VP. The territory model is
reviewed annually in January, and changes take effect on February 1.

## Territory Model

Territories are defined by region and segment. The regions are:
Americas (Austin-led), EMEA (London-led), and APAC (Singapore-led).
The segments are: enterprise (annual value above $100,000), mid-market
($25,000 to $100,000), and small business (below $25,000). Each
account is assigned to exactly one territory: one region and one
segment. Account executives own territories (a region-segment
combination, or a sub-region for enterprise), and account managers
own the renewals and expansion for existing accounts within the same
territory. New territories are created only in the January review,
except for hires mid-year, which split an existing territory.

## Assignment Rules

New accounts are assigned by sales operations within five business
days of the first inbound lead or meeting request. Assignment follows:
the lead source's region and segment, then the current load of the
territory owners (balanced by account count and pipeline value).
Inbound leads are assigned to the owning territory's account
executive; outbound leads are owned by the rep who sourced them and
are assigned to that rep's territory when the account is created. An
account can change territory only: when the segment changes (an
account growing above $100,000 moves to enterprise at the next
quarterly review), when the annual review reshapes territories, or
when the sales VP approves a transfer. Account changes are logged
with the account code (AC-####).

## Account Transfers

A transfer moves an account from one owner to another. Transfers
happen for: segment changes (with the quarterly review), territory
reshaping (February 1), manager direction (with the sales VP's
approval), and departures (the exiting rep's accounts are
reassigned within 5 business days of the departure date, per the
Termination and Offboarding Policy). Commission on transferred
accounts: the rep who closed the deal keeps the commission, and the
new owner earns commission on renewals and expansion from the
transfer date. A transfer in progress does not change the closing
owner: commission follows the account owner on the closing date, and
sales operations records the owner of record for every quote
(QT-####).

## Disputes

Assignment disputes (two reps claiming the same account, or a rep
claiming an account assigned elsewhere) are filed with sales
operations within 10 business days of the assignment or the
dispute event. Sales operations investigates the lead history and
the first-meeting record: the account belongs to the rep who logged
the first qualified meeting, unless the account was assigned by an
inbound lead, in which case the assignment wins. The sales VP
decides unresolved disputes within 5 business days. Disputes that
involve commission are resolved by the commission administrator
under the Sales Commission Plan, using the owner of record on the
closing date. Dispute decisions are final and are logged.

## Records and Reporting

The account assignment log records: the account, the owner, the
territory, the assignment date, and every change with its reason.
The log is the source of truth for commission and for pipeline
reporting. The territory review in January produces the territory
map for the year, and the map is distributed to the sales team on
February 1. Assignment records are retained for seven years under
the Data Retention Policy, and the quarterly segment review report
(accounts moving between segments) goes to the sales VP and
finance. The territory map feeds the sales forecast in the Budget
Planning and Forecasting cycle.

## Relationship to Other Policies

The Sales Commission Plan uses the owner of record from this
policy's log to pay commission, and its clawback rules apply to
transferred accounts. The Proposal and Quote Process assigns quotes
to the account owner. The Recruiting and Interview Policy covers
hires that create new territories. The Termination and Offboarding
Policy triggers the reassignment of departing reps' accounts. The
Partner Program Policy governs partner-sourced accounts, which are
assigned to a partner manager, not to the territory account
executive.
