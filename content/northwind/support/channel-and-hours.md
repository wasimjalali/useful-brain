# Channel and Hours Policy

## Support Channels

Northwind Systems supports customers through four channels: email,
chat, phone, and the customer portal. Email is support@northwind.example,
and email creates a ticket automatically. Chat is available in the
Meridian portal and on the website, and chat transcripts become
tickets when the conversation needs follow-up. Phone support is
available for P0 and P1 issues, and the number is listed in the
portal; phone calls are logged against the customer's account and
become tickets. The customer portal has the knowledge base, ticket
tracking, and the status page for incidents. All channels feed the
same ticket system, so a customer who emails, then calls, sees one
ticket history. The channels are operated by support, per the Ticket
Handling and Priority Policy.

## Coverage Hours

Coverage hours are 8:00 to 18:00 Central Time, Monday through Friday,
for email, chat, and the portal. Phone support for P0 and P1 issues
is available 24/7. Tickets logged outside coverage hours start their
SLA clock at 8:00 CT the next business day, except P0 and P1 tickets,
which are handled around the clock by the on-call rotation (see the
On-Call Rotation). The London office covers 8:00 to 17:00 London
time, which extends the coverage window for EMEA customers, and the
Singapore office covers 8:00 to 17:00 Singapore time for APAC
customers. The global coverage is scheduled so that at least one
region is staffed during every customer's business day, and the
coverage schedule is reviewed quarterly.

## Response Expectations by Channel

Email: first response within the Support SLA Policy targets (P2: 4
business hours, P3: 1 business day). Chat: a human responds within 5
minutes during coverage hours; if the agent cannot resolve within 30
minutes, the chat becomes a ticket and the SLA clock starts. Phone:
answered within 5 minutes for P0 and P1; if the queue is full, the
caller is called back within 30 minutes. Portal: submissions create
tickets with the same SLA as email. The knowledge base is available
24/7 and is the first-line self-service channel; articles follow the
Knowledge Base Article Guidelines. Customers who escalate a channel
issue (for example, an unanswered chat) are routed to the Customer
Complaint Escalation Path.

## Incident Communication

During P0 and P1 incidents, the status page in the portal is updated
within 1 hour of the incident being confirmed, per the Incident
Escalation Matrix. Affected customers receive an email with the
incident code (INC-####), the impact, and the next update time.
Updates go out every 4 hours until resolution, and a post-incident
summary is sent within 5 business days for SEV-1 and SEV-2 incidents.
Support agents cannot create incident communications themselves; they
are created by the incident commander and approved by the CISO or the
CTO. The status page is also used for scheduled maintenance notices,
which follow the Scheduled Maintenance Policy (72 hours notice, 24
hours customer notification).

## Out-of-Hours Coverage

Out-of-hours coverage exists for P0 and P1 issues only. The on-call
rotation (On-Call Rotation policy) carries the phone and the incident
pager, and the on-call support engineer responds within 15 minutes
for SEV-1 incidents. Non-urgent emails sent out of hours are answered
in the next coverage window. The escalation chain out of hours is:
on-call engineer, then the support manager on call, then the VP of
Support. The chain is published in the on-call calendar. Complaints
received out of hours are logged with their ESC-#### codes and start
their clocks at the next coverage window, because complaint response
times are measured in business hours.

## Holiday Coverage

Coverage on US, UK, and Singapore public holidays follows the local
office: the office closes and its channels move to the next office
that is open. The on-call rotation covers P0 and P1 across all
holidays, and the holiday on-call list is published 30 days in
advance. The December change freeze (December 15 to January 5, per
the Change Management Policy) does not change support hours; support
keeps normal coverage, and P0 tickets during the freeze still get the
15-minute response. The support manager reviews the holiday coverage
plan each quarter against the public holiday calendars of the three
offices.

## Relationship to Other Policies

The Support SLA Policy defines the response and resolution targets
that this policy's hours feed into. The Ticket Handling and Priority
Policy defines how tickets from each channel are classified. The
On-Call Rotation covers the out-of-hours staffing. The Incident
Escalation Matrix covers incident communication, and the Scheduled
Maintenance Policy covers maintenance notices. The Customer Complaint
Escalation Path covers complaints, which have their own channels and
times. The Office and Facilities Policy covers the physical offices
that the coverage schedule maps to.
