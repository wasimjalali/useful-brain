# Employee Expense Policy

## Scope

This policy covers business expenses incurred by Northwind Systems
employees and reimbursed by the company. It is the general expense
policy: the Travel Expense Policy overrides it for travel-specific items
(airfare, hotels, per-diem), and the Corporate Card Policy covers
purchases on company cards. Contractors are not covered here; their
rules are in the Contractor Expense Policy. Expense claims are submitted
through the expense system, which assigns each report a code in the
format EXP-####. The policy is owned by finance, and questions go to
expenses@northwind.example.

## What Is Reimbursable

Reimbursable expenses are ordinary and necessary business costs: office
supplies bought out of pocket, client meals, software subscriptions used
for work, books, parking, and work-related mileage. Personal expenses are
never reimbursable, including personal meals, commuting between home and
the office, and personal upgrades. Client meals are reimbursable up to
$150 per person including tax, with the client's name and business
purpose on the receipt. Subscriptions used for both work and personal
use are reimbursable only if the work use is the primary purpose, and
the finance team may ask for a justification. Gift cards are not
reimbursable in any amount; buying gift cards on a corporate card is a
violation of the Corporate Card Policy.

## Receipts and Documentation

Receipts are required for every expense of $25 or more. Under $25,
receipts are not required but the expense must still be itemized in the
report. A missing receipt for an expense over $25 can be replaced with a
signed statement from the employee explaining the purchase, but the
statement route is limited to three expenses per calendar year. Every
report line needs the business purpose, the date, and the attendees for
meals. The expense system flags reports missing required fields, and
finance returns incomplete reports within three business days instead of
approving them. Falsifying an expense is gross misconduct and is handled
under the Termination and Offboarding Policy.

## Submission Deadlines

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date, or
within 30 days of the trip's end for travel. Reports submitted after the
deadline are still reviewed but are flagged, and two late submissions in
a rolling 12-month period trigger a written reminder from finance. The
deadline exists so expenses land in the right accounting period; the
fiscal year closes on December 31, and expenses from the prior year must
be submitted by January 31 to be paid. Reimbursement is paid within 10
business days of approval, in the next payroll run. Approval happens
within five business days of submission, so the full cycle from
submission to payment is at most 15 business days when the report is
clean.

## Approval Thresholds

Approval authority is based on the total of the report: managers approve
reports up to $2,500, department VPs approve reports from $2,500 to
$10,000, and the CFO approves reports above $10,000. Approvers cannot
approve their own reports; a manager's own report goes to their manager,
and the CFO's reports are approved by the CEO. Reports above $10,000
need a business case attached. The thresholds apply to the report total,
not to individual lines, so a report with ten $300 lines still needs VP
approval. Approval is not rubber-stamping: approvers review the business
purpose, and finance audits a sample of 10% of approved reports each
quarter.

## Mileage and Out-of-Pocket

Mileage for personal vehicles used on business is reimbursed at $0.65 per
mile, the same rate used by the Travel Expense Policy. Mileage is
calculated by the expense system from the addresses entered; manually
entered distances above 50 miles need manager approval. Out-of-pocket
purchases for the company (for example, paying a vendor COD) are
reimbursed under the same rules, and any single out-of-pocket purchase
above $500 should have been approved in advance by the manager. Currency:
expenses in foreign currency are converted at the monthly finance rate,
and the system shows the rate used on the report.

## Relationship to Other Policies

Expenses for travel use the rates in the Travel Expense Policy: per-diem
meals of $65/$85/$75, hotel caps of $250/$350/$300, and the airfare
rules. Training expenses follow the Training and Development Budget
($2,500 annual allocation, manager approval). Reimbursement for departing
employees is included in the final pay process of the Termination and
Offboarding Policy, subject to the same deadlines. Corporate card
holders follow the Corporate Card Policy, which requires the same
receipts and deadlines; card expenses do not need a separate
reimbursement because they are paid directly by finance.
