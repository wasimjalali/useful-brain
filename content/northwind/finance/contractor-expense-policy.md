# Contractor Expense Policy

## Scope

This policy covers expenses incurred by contractors, consultants, and
temporary staff working with Northwind Systems. Contractors are not
employees, and their expense rules differ from the Employee Expense
Policy in three important ways: reimbursement takes 30 business days,
approval is by finance only (no manager approval chain), and the receipt
threshold is $50 instead of $25. This policy is administered by finance
and is shared with operations because operations manages contractor
agreements and vendor onboarding. Questions go to expenses@northwind.example
with the contractor's invoice code.

## Reimbursable Expenses

Reimbursable contractor expenses are limited to those written into the
contractor's agreement or statement of work: travel to Northwind sites
or customer sites, project-specific software licenses, and materials
directly consumed by the engagement. Expenses not in the agreement are
not reimbursable, even if an employee sponsor approves them informally.
Contractors do not receive per-diem meal rates: meals are reimbursed at
actual cost with receipts, capped at $60 per day, and only when the
contractor is traveling away from their home base. Client entertainment
by contractors is not reimbursable unless the agreement explicitly
includes it. The employee sponsor (the person managing the contractor)
can answer questions about what the agreement covers.

## Receipts and Documentation

Receipts are required for every expense of $50 or more. Expenses under
$50 do not need a receipt but must be itemized. There is no signed
statement route for missing receipts; a missing receipt over $50 makes
that line non-reimbursable. Each claim line needs the date, purpose,
and the agreement code (CT-####) from the contractor's contract. Claims
are submitted through the contractor portal, not the employee expense
system. Falsified claims are a breach of the contractor agreement and
lead to termination of the engagement and removal from the vendor list
under the Vendor and Procurement Policy.

## Submission and Payment Timing

Contractors submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense date.
Claims are paid within 30 business days of approval, not 10 business
days like employee expenses, because contractor payments run through the
vendor payment cycle with a single monthly run. The finance team approves
or rejects claims within five business days of submission. Rejected
claims come back with the reason and can be resubmitted once, corrected,
within 10 business days. Payment is made by bank transfer to the
contractor's registered account, and the Contractor Expense Policy does
not offer payroll-run reimbursement or corporate cards. Late claims (over
30 days) are paid in the following month's run without penalty but are
flagged on the contractor's account.

## Approval and Controls

All contractor expense approvals are made by finance staff; the employee
sponsor's role is to confirm the expense relates to the engagement, and
finance makes the payment decision. The finance manager approves claims
up to $2,500, and the finance director approves claims above $2,500;
there is no VP or CFO step because the claim total is capped by the
agreement's expense limit, which is set at contract signing and
registered in the vendor system. Contractor claims above $5,000 in a
single month are reviewed by the CFO as part of the vendor payment run.
Contractors cannot hold corporate cards under any circumstances, and
the Corporate Card Policy does not apply to them.

## Expenses for Contractors at Northwind Sites

Contractors working at Northwind offices follow the Office and Facilities
Policy for access and safety, but their expense claims still follow this
policy. Meals at the office cafeteria or canteen are not reimbursable.
If a contractor needs equipment (laptop, badge, access card), it is
provisioned by operations through the vendor system and returned when
the engagement ends, following the same 30-day return rule as the
Equipment Return Policy; lost or damaged equipment is charged against
the contractor's final invoice. Contractors are not eligible for the
training budget, the referral program, or any employee benefit in the
Employee Handbook.

## Relationship to Other Policies

Contractor invoices for services are paid under the Invoicing and
Payment Terms Policy's Net 30 terms and the Vendor and Procurement
Policy's approval thresholds. Contractors who also receive employee
referral bonuses or commissions must not: those payments are for
employees only. If a contractor is later hired as an employee, their
expense rules change on their first day of employment, and any claims
pending at that point are paid under the contractor rules to keep the
audit trail clean. Disputes about contractor expenses follow the
Dispute Resolution and Arbitration Policy like any other contract
dispute.
