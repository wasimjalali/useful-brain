# Travel Expense Policy

## Booking Travel

All business travel for Northwind Systems is booked through the corporate
travel portal, which applies negotiated rates for flights, hotels, and
trains. Booking outside the portal requires manager approval and a
written reason. Flights should be booked at least 14 days in advance
wherever the schedule allows; last-minute bookings are allowed but the
portal flags them for review. Travel is a business expense, and the
general rules of the Employee Expense Policy apply to everything this
policy does not override: expense reports within 30 days, receipts at
$25 and above, and reimbursement within 10 business days of approval.
Travel requests need manager approval before booking; trips estimated
above $5,000 need the department director's approval in addition.

## Airfare

Economy class is the standard for all flights under six hours of flying
time. Business class is permitted for flights of six hours or more, and
for any flight where the traveler has a documented medical need. Business
class fares above $1,500 require VP approval before booking; the portal
enforces this. Upgrades paid out of pocket are the traveler's own expense
and are not reimbursed. Ancillary fees (checked baggage, seat selection
on flights over four hours) are reimbursable. Flight changes caused by
Northwind are reimbursed in full; changes caused by the traveler are
reimbursed up to $150 in change fees per trip, with the rest at the
traveler's cost.

## Hotels and Accommodation

Hotel caps are $250 per night in the US, $350 per night in London, and
$300 per night in Singapore, excluding taxes. Rates above the cap are
reimbursed only with manager pre-approval, and the portal shows which
hotels are within the cap at booking time. The cap is per night, not per
trip. For conferences, the cap is raised by 20% during the conference
dates if the conference hotel is above the cap, but only for the nights
of the conference. Extended stays (more than 14 consecutive nights) are
reimbursed at 80% of the cap because weekly rates are lower; the traveler
books the weekly rate in the portal.

## Per-Diem Meals

Per-diem meals are paid as a fixed daily amount, no receipts required:
$65 per day in the US, $85 per day in London, and $75 per day in
Singapore. The per-diem covers all meals for a full travel day. Partial
travel days are prorated: 50% of the daily rate for a day that includes
either departure or arrival, 100% only when both departure and arrival
fall within the travel window. Meals provided by a client, conference, or
Northwind event are not additionally claimable that day. The per-diem is
the same for all employees regardless of level; it is not available to
contractors, who follow the Contractor Expense Policy.

## Ground Transportation and Mileage

Rideshare and taxi costs are reimbursed with receipts. Personal vehicle
mileage is reimbursed at $0.65 per mile, and the mileage rate is
reviewed annually by finance. Parking and tolls are reimbursed with
receipts. Rental cars are allowed when public transport is not practical;
the standard rental class is economy or compact, and insurance through
the rental company is reimbursed because Northwind's corporate policy
does not cover personal-name rentals. International driving requires the
traveler to hold a valid local or international permit, and rentals
abroad need manager approval.

## Travel Approval and Safety

Manager approval is required for every trip before booking. International
travel needs an additional approval from the department head, and travel
to countries on the restricted destinations list (maintained by
operations) needs the COO's approval. Northwind provides travel
insurance covering medical and trip cancellation for all business
travel; travelers do not need to buy separate policies, and personal
add-ons are not reimbursed. The Office and Facilities Policy covers
visitor safety at our offices, and the Business Continuity Policy covers
what to do during a travel disruption or emergency abroad: contact the
operations duty line, and follow the incident process.

## Expense Submission

Travel expenses are submitted through the expense system as a single
report per trip, attached to the travel request code (TR-####), within
30 days of the trip's end. Reimbursement is paid within 10 business days
of approval, in the next payroll run. The Employee Expense Policy's
approval thresholds apply: manager approves up to $2,500, VP approves
$2,500 to $10,000, and the CFO approves anything above $10,000. Travel
that was pre-approved but not taken (for example, a cancelled conference)
is not claimable except for non-refundable booking fees, which are
reimbursed with the cancellation notice. Personal travel added to a
business trip is separated on the report and paid by the employee.
