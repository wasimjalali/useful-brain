# Corporate Card Policy

## Issuance and Eligibility

Corporate cards at Northwind Systems are issued to employees whose role
requires regular business purchasing: managers, directors, and finance
staff. Cards are issued by finance and are the property of the company.
Requests for a card are submitted by the manager to finance with a
business justification; the finance manager approves or denies within
five business days. Cards are issued with the cardholder's name and a
card code in the format CC-####. Contractors can never hold corporate
cards, per the Contractor Expense Policy. A cardholder who changes role
or leaves the company returns the card on their last day, under the
Termination and Offboarding Policy's equipment rules.

## Spending Limits

Monthly spending limits are set by role: $10,000 per month for standard
cardholders (managers), $25,000 per month for directors, and $50,000
per month for VPs and above. Finance staff receive the limit matching
their role. The limit resets on the first of each month and applies to
the calendar month, not a rolling 30 days. A single purchase above
$5,000 requires pre-approval by the finance manager, even if the monthly
limit would cover it. Temporary limit increases (up to 60 days) are
approved by the finance director. The card system blocks purchases that
would exceed the monthly limit; there is no overdraft.

## Permitted and Prohibited Purchases

Cards are for business purchases only: software subscriptions, client
meals, travel booked outside the portal in approved cases, office
supplies, and conference fees. Personal purchases are strictly
prohibited at any amount, including personal meals, personal fuel, and
personal travel. Gift cards are prohibited in any amount and are
grounds for immediate card revocation and disciplinary review. Cash
advances are not available on corporate cards. Split purchases (splitting
one purchase into multiple transactions to stay under a limit or
approval threshold) are prohibited. Alcohol purchases are permitted for
client entertainment up to $100 per event; anything beyond that needs
manager pre-approval.

## Expense Reporting for Card Purchases

Card purchases are imported into the expense system automatically, and
the cardholder must allocate and document each transaction within 30
days of the purchase date, with receipts at $25 and above, exactly like
the Employee Expense Policy. Undocumented transactions are flagged at
day 30 and the card is blocked at day 45 until the report is completed.
Card expenses do not require reimbursement (finance pays the card
directly) but they require approval under the same thresholds: manager
approval up to $2,500, VP from $2,500 to $10,000, CFO above $10,000.
Personal charges found on a card are recovered from the cardholder's
pay under the payroll deduction process and reported to the CHRO.

## Card Security and Loss

Cardholders are responsible for the security of their card and its
details. Report a lost or stolen card immediately to finance and block
it in the card portal; the card is blocked within minutes, and a
replacement arrives in 5 to 7 business days. The cardholder is not
liable for fraudulent charges made after the loss is reported. Card
details are never stored in personal password managers; they live in
the corporate vault. Suspected fraud on a statement is disputed through
the card portal within 10 business days of the statement date, and
finance tracks disputes to resolution. Sharing card details with anyone,
including colleagues, is prohibited; each cardholder has their own card.

## Card Reviews and Audits

Finance reviews card statements monthly and audits a sample of
cardholders each quarter for compliance with this policy. The audit
checks: documentation completeness, business purpose, limit adherence,
and prohibited purchase categories. Findings are sent to the cardholder
and their manager. Repeated violations (two in a rolling 12-month
period) lead to card revocation for 90 days; a third violation leads to
permanent revocation and a note in the employee's HR record. The
quarterly audit results are summarized for the CFO and included in the
April and October SOC 2 audit evidence, per the Regulatory Compliance
Calendar.

## Cards for Offsite and Travel

Cards are the preferred payment method for business travel: the Travel
Expense Policy's rules apply to travel purchases on cards, including the
per-diem (which is paid as reimbursement, never as a card cash advance),
hotel caps, and airfare class rules. Travel booked through the corporate
travel portal is paid by finance directly; cards are for incidental
travel costs. International purchases are converted at the card
processor's rate, and the difference from the monthly finance rate is
not reimbursable. Card rewards accrue to the company, never to the
cardholder, and using a personal card to earn rewards on business
purchases is permitted but the expenses still follow the Employee
Expense Policy.
