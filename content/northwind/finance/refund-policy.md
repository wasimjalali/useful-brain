# Refund Policy

## Overview

This policy governs refunds for Northwind Core, Atlas, and Meridian
subscriptions. It is owned by finance and used by support and sales, who
are the teams customers talk to. Refunds are not the same as returns:
this policy covers money back for software subscriptions. Hardware and
equipment are covered by the Equipment Return Policy, which has different
windows and approvers. Every refund is processed through a support ticket
and gets a request code in the format RF-####. Customers never contact
finance directly; they request refunds through support, and support
routes the request to the finance team.

## Refund Windows

Refund windows are measured in calendar days from the invoice date. For
annual plans, a customer may request a refund within 14 calendar days of
the invoice date. For monthly plans, the window is 7 calendar days. A
request inside the window is granted automatically if the account has not
violated the terms of service; the refund is the full amount paid if the
service was not used, or a pro-rated amount if usage occurred, computed
from the invoice date to the day the request was logged. Requests
outside the window are denied unless they qualify under the exceptions
in this policy. The window starts at the invoice date, not the contract
start date, and not the date the customer noticed the charge.

## Approval Authority

Refunds up to $5,000 are approved by the finance manager on the billing
team. Refunds above $5,000 require the CFO's approval, and the CFO
reviews refunds over $5,000 weekly in the billing review. Support agents
cannot approve refunds at any amount; they can only log the request and
attach the customer's reason. The sales team can recommend a refund (for
example, as part of a retention conversation), but the decision is
finance's. Refunds over $25,000 in a single calendar month to one
customer trigger a review by the CFO and the General Counsel, Amelia
Brooks, under the Dispute Resolution and Arbitration Policy.

## Refund Processing Time

Once approved, the refund is processed within 5 to 10 business days. The
money returns to the original payment method: card refunds go back to the
card, bank transfers back to the bank account on file. For annual plans
paid by invoice, the refund is issued as a credit against the next
invoice unless the customer requests a transfer. The refund shows on the
customer's next billing statement, and finance sends a confirmation email
from refunds@northwind.example with the RF-#### code. If the original
payment method is no longer valid, finance contacts the customer for an
alternative; if none is provided within 30 days, the refund is held as
account credit.

## Exceptions

Three exceptions allow refunds outside the window. First, statutory
rights: customers in jurisdictions with a cooling-off period longer than
14 days receive the local statutory window, and the support team applies
it automatically for UK customers (14 working days from contract
acceptance). Second, billing errors: a charge that was not authorized,
was duplicated, or was billed at the wrong rate is corrected in full,
with no window, once the billing error is confirmed by finance. Third,
service failure: if a customer experienced a documented SEV-1 or SEV-2
incident (see the Incident Escalation Matrix) that prevented use of the
product for more than 48 hours, they can request a pro-rated refund for
the affected period, regardless of the window.

## Refund and Data

When a refund is granted for a full plan termination, the customer's data
is scheduled for deletion 30 days after the refund is processed, under
the Privacy Policy's deletion rules, and the account moves to the
offboarding checklist. Customers can request earlier deletion through
the Customer Data Access Requests process (DSAR-####, 30-day response
window). Refunds do not pause data retention: the Data Retention Policy
still applies, and invoice records are kept for seven years. A refunded
customer who re-subscribes within 90 days does not regain any prior
usage history; the account starts fresh.

## Refund Denials and Appeals

Denied refunds are explained in writing with the reason and the policy
reference. The customer can appeal once, through support, within 14 days
of the denial; the appeal is reviewed by the finance manager, not the
person who denied the request. If the appeal is denied, the customer's
remaining option is the Customer Complaint Escalation Path (ESC-3 and
above involve the VP of Support and, if the amount is over $2,500, the
goodwill limits of that policy). Legal disputes about refunds follow the
Dispute Resolution and Arbitration Policy. All denials are logged in the
billing system so the CFO can see denial patterns by product and plan.
