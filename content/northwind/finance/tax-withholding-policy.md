# Tax Withholding Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains how Northwind Systems withholds and reports taxes on
payroll and invoices in its three locations: Austin, London, and
Singapore. It is the employee-facing summary; the detailed filing
calendar is in the Regulatory Compliance Calendar, and the detailed
rules are administered by finance and payroll. Employees do not need to
calculate withholding themselves, but they should know what is withheld,
how to change it, and where to ask questions (payroll@northwind.example).
The policy follows local law in each office; where this summary and local
law differ, local law wins.

## US Payroll Withholding (Austin)

US employees have federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare, and
state income tax (Texas has no state income tax, so Austin employees see
federal withholding only) deducted from each paycheck. New hires complete
federal withholding forms in their first week; the forms can be updated
at any time in the payroll portal, and changes take effect within two
pay periods. Northwind matches Social Security and Medicare
contributions at the statutory rates. Payroll runs twice a month, on the
15th and the last business day of the month. Year-end tax forms are
available in the payroll portal by January 31 of the following year, and
the deadline is enforced by the payroll provider.

## UK Payroll Withholding (London)

London employees are paid under the UK PAYE system: income tax and
National Insurance are withheld by Northwind and reported to the tax
authority each pay period. Payroll runs monthly, on the last business
day of the month. Employees receive payslips through the payroll portal,
and year-end summaries are provided by April 30 following the tax year
(April 6 to April 5). New hires provide their tax code at onboarding;
changes come from the tax authority and are applied automatically.
Expenses reimbursed under the Employee Expense Policy are not taxable
when they follow the approved rates in the Travel Expense Policy;
per-diem paid above the approved rates would be taxable, so the
approved rates must be used.

## Singapore Payroll Withholding (Singapore)

Singapore employees have Central Provident Fund (CPF) contributions
deducted from salary, with the employer contribution added on top, at
the statutory rates by age band. Income tax in Singapore is collected
annually by assessment, not by monthly withholding, so Northwind does
not withhold income tax from monthly pay; employees receive their
assessments directly. Payroll runs monthly on the last business day.
CPF contributions are due by the 14th of the following month, and late
contributions incur penalties that are the employee's and company's
joint responsibility to avoid. New hires register for CPF within their
first month.

## Withholding on Vendor and Contractor Payments

Contractor payments are not payroll: contractors invoice Northwind and
are paid under the Contractor Expense Policy and the Invoicing and
Payment Terms Policy. US-based contractors complete a tax information
form at engagement start; Northwind reports payments above $600 in a
calendar year to the tax authority and provides the contractor a copy by
January 31. Contractors are responsible for their own tax payments;
Northwind does not withhold on contractor invoices except where local
law requires. UK and Singapore contractors follow the local rules, and
the Vendor and Procurement Policy requires a valid tax registration
number on file before the first invoice is paid.

## Taxes on Invoices to Customers

Customer invoices are issued without sales tax for B2B software
subscriptions in the US, UK, and Singapore where the customer is a
business with a valid registration number on file, which billing
collects at contract signing. Where tax applies (for example, consumer
sales or customers without a registration number), it is added to the
invoice at the applicable rate and remitted per the Regulatory
Compliance Calendar. Tax rates on invoices are set by the billing
system from the customer's billing address and the product type; the
billing team does not manually set rates. The Refund Policy's refund of
an invoice includes the tax portion of the invoice, refunded with the
same processing window of 5 to 10 business days.

## Questions and Corrections

Payroll questions go to payroll@northwind.example, and the payroll team
answers within three business days. Withholding errors are corrected in
the next payroll run if reported within 90 days; errors reported later
are corrected in the following month's run. Employees who believe too
much or too little was withheld should check their withholding forms
first, then contact payroll. Tax forms, payslips, and year-end documents
are available in the payroll portal for the current year and the prior
year; older records are archived per the Data Retention Policy (HR
records six years, financial records seven years). The annual tax
calendar is published each January in the Regulatory Compliance
Calendar.
