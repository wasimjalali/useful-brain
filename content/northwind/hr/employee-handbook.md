# Employee Handbook

## Welcome to Northwind Systems

Northwind Systems builds business software for mid-sized companies. We are
a B2B software company of approximately 400 employees, founded in 2014,
with headquarters in Austin, Texas, and offices in London and Singapore.
Our products are Northwind Core, our main operations platform; Atlas, our
data integration product; and Meridian, our customer portal. This handbook
is the entry point to company policy: it summarizes the rules you need day
to day and points to the full policy documents, which are the source of
truth. If anything here conflicts with a policy document, the policy
document wins, and the handbook is updated to match. Questions go to
hr@northwind.example.

## Employment Basics

Employment is at-will in the United States; notice periods, probation,
and statutory rights in the UK and Singapore follow local law and the
employment contract. New hires complete a 90-day check-in with their
manager, then join the normal annual review cycle (reviews March 1-31,
decisions by April 30, changes effective May 1, per the Performance
Review Policy). Levels run from L1 to L8, and promotions take effect the
first day of the quarter after approval. Your manager is your first stop
for questions about your role; HR handles anything about pay, leave, or
workplace conduct.

## Working Hours and Remote Work

Core working hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. You may work remotely
up to three days per week with manager approval, per the Remote Work and
Flexible Hours Policy. Working abroad for more than 90 consecutive days
needs HR approval in advance. All-hands days are the first Thursday of
each month and are office days. Support and operations roles follow their
own coverage schedules (see the Channel and Hours Policy and the On-Call
Rotation). Nobody in a non-support role records hours; we track outcomes,
not time.

## Leave and Time Off

Annual leave is 20 days in Austin, 25 days in London, and 18 days in
Singapore, plus public holidays. You may carry over up to five days, and
carried days must be used by March 31. Sick leave is ten days per year.
You also get five bereavement days per event, jury duty leave, and a
four-week paid sabbatical after seven years of service. Parental leave is
a separate entitlement: sixteen weeks paid, per parent, for birth,
adoption, and surrogacy, and it must start within twelve months of the
child joining the family. Full details are in the Leave and Time Off
Policy and the Parental Leave Policy. Request leave in the HR portal; it
shows up as LOA-#### or PL-#### codes.

## Expenses and Travel

Business expenses are reimbursed through the Employee Expense Policy:
submit reports within 30 days, receipts are required at $25 and above, and
reimbursement lands in your next payroll run within 10 business days of
approval. Travel is booked per the Travel Expense Policy: economy for
flights under six hours, business class above that (with VP approval when
the fare exceeds $1,500), per-diem meals at $65 in the US, $85 in London,
and $75 in Singapore, and hotel caps of $250, $350, and $300 per night
respectively. Managers approve expenses under $2,500; larger amounts go up
the chain. Contractors have separate rules: 30 business days to
reimburse, receipts at $50 and above.

## Conduct and Confidentiality

Treat customers, colleagues, and partners with respect. Harassment of any
kind is prohibited and is reported through the Anti-Harassment Policy;
reports go to HR or the CHRO and are investigated within ten business
days. Confidential information, including customer data, is protected
under the IP and Confidentiality Policy: do not discuss it in public
spaces, and use a privacy screen when handling customer data remotely.
Security incidents are reported through the Incident Escalation Matrix
(SEV-1 through SEV-4). Insider information about customers or partners is
not shared outside Northwind under any circumstances.

## Customers and Support

Customer-facing staff follow the Support SLA Policy (P0 tickets get a
15-minute response, 24/7), the Customer Complaint Escalation Path
(ESC-1 through ESC-4), and the Customer Onboarding Checklist for new
accounts. Refunds follow the Refund Policy: annual plans can be refunded
within 14 calendar days of the invoice, monthly plans within 7 days.
Sales staff follow the Discount Approval Policy (discounts above 20%
need the CFO), the Proposal and Quote Process, and the Referral Program.
Everyone who talks to customers uses the Customer Data Access Requests
process for data requests, which have a 30-day response window.

## Facilities and Security

Badges are required in all offices, and visitors must be signed in (see
the Office and Facilities Policy). Company equipment is returned within
30 days of your last day, per the Equipment Return Policy. Scheduled
maintenance happens Saturday 02:00-04:00 UTC, and production changes go
out Tuesday or Thursday 10:00-14:00 CT, except during the change freeze
from December 15 to January 5. Report physical security issues to
facilities and information security issues to security@northwind.example.
The Business Continuity Policy explains what happens in an emergency:
critical systems return to service within four hours, and data loss is
capped at 30 minutes.
