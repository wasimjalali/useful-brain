# Remote Work and Flexible Hours Policy

## Core Hours

Northwind Systems runs on core hours in each office: employees are
expected to be available between 10:00 and 15:00 local time, Monday
through Friday. Outside those hours, schedules are flexible. The core
hours exist so teams in Austin, London, and Singapore can hold meetings
and reviews; teams that work across offices agree their own meeting
windows within each office's core hours. Support roles follow the
Channel and Hours Policy instead of this one, because their coverage
windows are fixed. Attendance at the weekly team meeting (held Tuesday
10:30 local time) is expected for all roles covered by this policy.

## Remote Days

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, with manager
approval. The approval is a standing arrangement: one approval covers
the pattern, and the employee does not need per-day sign-off. Roles that
must be on-site include: facilities staff, IT hardware support, and
anyone handling physical mail or visitor management. Remote days are not
a substitute for leave: working remotely is still work, and leave must
be booked through the Leave and Time Off Policy. Managers can ask a
team to come in on a scheduled remote day for an all-hands or a
customer visit, with at least three business days of notice.

## Long-Term Remote and Working Abroad

Working remotely from outside your home office's country for more than
90 consecutive days requires HR approval before you go. HR reviews
visa, tax, and insurance implications; the review takes up to three
weeks. Without approval, working abroad beyond 90 days is treated as
unauthorized absence and may result in disciplinary action under the
Termination and Offboarding Policy. Up to 90 days abroad needs only
manager approval, but the manager must log it in the HR portal.
Employees on parental leave returning under a phased return follow the
same rules: a phased return is 80% time on 100% pay for up to four
weeks, and the remote-day rules apply from the return date.

## Equipment and Connectivity

Remote work equipment is provided by IT: a laptop, a headset, and a
second monitor on request. For home connectivity, Northwind reimburses
up to $60 per month for internet, claimed through the Employee Expense
Policy with a receipt, under the general $25 receipt threshold rules.
Employees are responsible for a workspace that meets the security
requirements in the Security and Vulnerability Policy: a locked room or
a privacy screen when handling customer data, and no work in public
spaces with customer data visible. IT runs a remote-work security
checklist once a year, and failing it twice triggers a review of the
remote arrangement.

## Flexible Schedules

Compressed work weeks (four days at 10 hours) are available with manager
approval, and are reviewed annually. Core hours still apply on working
days. Schedule changes of up to two hours at either end of the day need
only manager agreement; anything larger needs the department head's
approval because it affects coverage. Parents returning from parental
leave can request a fixed schedule (for example, 9:00 to 16:00) for up
to six months after their return; the request cannot be refused without
a written business reason from the department head. Time tracking is by
outcome, not by hours: nobody in a role covered by this policy records
hours, and overtime rules do not apply except where local law requires.

## Office Expectations

The days you are in the office are yours to choose within the three-day
remote cap, but teams are encouraged to align on shared office days
(Wednesday is the default shared day in all three offices). Desk
booking is through the facilities portal, and the hot-desking rules are
in the Office and Facilities Policy. Visitors from other offices use
the same booking system and are entitled to a desk for up to five
consecutive days without approval; longer stays need the local office
manager's approval. All-hands days (first Thursday of each month) are
office days for everyone, and remote participation is not available
except for employees on approved long-term remote arrangements.
