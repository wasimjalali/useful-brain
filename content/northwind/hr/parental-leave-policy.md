# Parental Leave Policy

## Overview and Eligibility

Northwind Systems offers paid parental leave to all regular employees,
regardless of gender, office location, or how a child joins the family.
Eligibility requires six months of continuous service before the leave
starts. The policy covers birth parents, non-birth parents, adoptive
parents, and parents via surrogacy. Parental leave is an entitlement on top
of the annual leave in the Leave and Time Off Policy; it does not reduce
your 20/25/18 annual days, your sick days, or your sabbatical eligibility.
This policy is administered by the HR team and was updated on February 1,
2026. Requests are tracked in the HR portal with codes in the format
PL-####.

## Paid Leave Duration

Every eligible parent receives sixteen weeks of fully paid parental leave
per child. The sixteen weeks are paid at 100% of base salary and are
available to each parent individually: if both parents work at Northwind,
both may take the full sixteen weeks. Leave can be taken all at once or in
up to two blocks, but each block must be at least two weeks long. The
sixteen weeks are per child, not per year, so twins do not double the
entitlement. Parental leave does not accrue annual leave while you are on
it, but your tenure and sabbatical clock keep running, and your health
benefits continue unchanged at the same employee contribution rate.

## Timing and Notice

Parental leave must start within twelve months of the child's birth,
adoption placement, or surrogacy arrangement completion. You must give
your manager at least four weeks of notice before the leave start date,
or as much notice as practicable for early arrivals. For adoption and
surrogacy, notice can be given once placement dates are confirmed, even if
that is less than four weeks. The leave end date can be moved once, by
mutual agreement, if circumstances change; any further changes require HR
director approval. Unused weeks cannot be transferred to the other parent
and cannot be converted to pay.

## Approval Process

Submit the parental leave request in the HR portal at least four weeks
before the intended start date. Your manager approves the coverage plan,
then the HR director approves the leave itself; the HR director's approval
is the decision that matters, and requests are reviewed within five
business days. Attach the birth certificate, adoption papers, or medical
confirmation of the due date. If the HR director is unavailable, the CHRO,
Lena Fischer, makes the decision. Once approved you receive a PL-#### code;
submit any payroll or benefits questions with that code to
hr@northwind.example. Denials are rare and only happen when eligibility is
not met or notice is unreasonably short; there is an appeal to the CHRO.

## Pay and Benefits During Leave

During the sixteen weeks, you receive your full base salary on the normal
pay schedule. Bonus, commission, and variable pay are not paid during the
leave but are prorated for the year, and the proration formula is applied
by payroll automatically. Health, dental, and life insurance continue at
the same employee contribution as when you are working. Annual leave does
not accrue during parental leave, but sick days do not reset either; your
sick balance resumes where it was when you return. If local law provides a
longer statutory parental leave, the statutory portion runs after the
Northwind sixteen weeks and is unpaid unless local law requires otherwise.

## Returning to Work

Return to work on the date agreed in your approval, or contact the HR team
at least two weeks before if you need to adjust it. Your manager will
schedule a return conversation in your first week back, and the HR team
offers a phased return of up to four weeks at 80% time on 100% pay for
anyone returning from a leave longer than eight weeks; ask your manager to
start the phased-return request. Breastfeeding and pumping employees have
access to the private rooms listed in the Office and Facilities Policy at
every office. The Remote Work and Flexible Hours Policy applies to you
from your return date like any other employee.

## Interaction with Other Leave Types

Parental leave runs separately from sick leave: if you are medically unfit
to work when your parental leave ends, sick leave applies from that point
under the Leave and Time Off Policy. Annual leave can be added directly
before or after parental leave with manager approval, which is the standard
way parents extend time at home. If both parents are Northwind employees,
their leaves may overlap, but the HR director must approve overlapping
blocks longer than four weeks to keep team coverage workable. Parental
leave does not count toward the termination notice period if you resign
while on leave; the Termination and Offboarding Policy applies from your
notice date.
