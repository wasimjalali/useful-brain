# Salary Bands

## Purpose and Confidentiality

This document defines the salary bands for all Northwind Systems roles.
It is confidential HR data: it is visible only to the HR department, and
sharing band ranges with anyone outside HR is a disciplinary matter up to
and including termination. Individual salaries are never published even
inside HR; bands are ranges, and payroll staff see actual figures only on a
need-to-know basis. The bands below are in USD for Austin (the home market).
Questions about the bands go to the CHRO, Lena Fischer. This document is
owned by HR and reviewed annually in March, ahead of the April compensation
review cycle.

## Level Framework

Every role maps to a level from L1 to L8. Levels are about scope and
seniority, not tenure: an L5 individual contributor and an L6 first-line
manager are different levels even when the manager is younger in service.
The level definitions are: L1 entry-level and internships converted to
full-time, L2 early-career, L3 established individual contributor, L4
senior individual contributor, L5 principal individual contributor or
senior specialist, L6 manager or lead, L7 director, L8 vice president and
above, including C-suite. Level assignment is documented in the
Performance Review Policy, and promotion timing is covered by that policy's
promotion rules, effective the first day of the quarter after approval.

## Salary Bands

Annual base salary ranges for Austin-based roles, in USD:

| Level | Band minimum | Band maximum |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | $60,000 | $75,000 |
| L2 | $75,000 | $95,000 |
| L3 | $95,000 | $120,000 |
| L4 | $120,000 | $150,000 |
| L5 | $150,000 | $190,000 |
| L6 | $170,000 | $220,000 |
| L7 | $210,000 | $280,000 |
| L8 | $260,000 | $350,000 |

Bands are annual base salary only. They exclude bonus targets (typically
5% at L1-L2, 10% at L3-L5, 15% at L6-L7, 25% at L8), equity grants, and
any location adjustment. Offers and raises must land inside the band for
the level; a candidate or employee cannot be paid outside these ranges
without the exception process in this document.

## Geographic Adjustments

The bands above apply to Austin. London roles are adjusted upward by 12%
to account for the market, and Singapore roles are adjusted downward by
10%. Adjustments apply to the band endpoints, not to individual pay: a
London L4 sits in a $134,400-$168,000 band, and a Singapore L4 sits in a
$108,000-$135,000 band. The adjustment is applied at hire and re-checked
each March during the band review; it does not change when exchange rates
move mid-year. Anyone transferring between offices is repriced into the
destination band at the same level, which can mean a pay change on the
transfer date.

## Band Review and Effective Dates

Bands are reviewed every March and any changes are effective May 1 of the
same year, which is also the effective date for compensation changes that
come out of the performance review cycle (decisions communicated by April
30). The March review is done by HR with finance input and approved by the
CHRO and the CFO, Marcus Webb. Band changes are not raises: an individual's
pay only moves through the normal review or promotion process. When a band
minimum rises above an employee's current pay, HR flags the employee for a
catch-up adjustment at the next review, effective May 1.

## Exceptions and Approvals

Paying above a band maximum requires written approval from both the CFO
and the CHRO, and is granted only for critical hires or retention cases;
approvals are reviewed every six months and must be renewed. Paying below
a band minimum is not permitted at hire; if an employee is below minimum
after a band change, they get a mandatory review within six months.
Promotion into a higher band follows the Performance Review Policy's
promotion rules and takes effect the first day of the next quarter. Offers
outside the bands made by recruiting are rejected by HR before they reach
the candidate, per the Recruiting and Interview Policy's offer approval
steps.

## Data Handling

Band data lives in the HR system and in the annual March review workbook.
It is never placed in documents accessible to other departments, never
quoted in offer letters (offers state a figure, not a band), and never
included in manager dashboards. Employees can see their own level and their
own pay, but not ranges, on the HR portal. Requests for band data from
outside HR are refused and routed to the CHRO. Audit access to the March
review workbook is logged and reviewed by the CHRO quarterly.
