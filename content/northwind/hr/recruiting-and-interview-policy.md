# Recruiting and Interview Policy

## Roles and Responsibilities

Recruiting at Northwind Systems is owned by the HR team, which runs
sourcing, scheduling, and the offer process. The hiring manager owns the
decision. This policy applies to everyone who interviews candidates,
which is why it is shared with engineering and sales in addition to HR.
Interviewers are trained before they interview; untrained staff cannot sit
on interview panels. Every role opening is created in the recruiting
system with a req code in the format REQ-####, and all interview feedback
is recorded in that system within one business day of the interview.
Feedback is never shared with the candidate.

## Interview Loop

The standard interview loop is four rounds maximum: a screening call with
the recruiter (30 minutes), a technical or functional interview with a
peer (45 minutes), a panel interview with the hiring manager and two team
members (60 minutes), and a final interview with the department head (30
minutes). Loops are capped at four rounds; a fifth round requires the
department VP's approval and happens only for director-level roles. All
rounds are scheduled between 9:00 and 17:00 local time, and candidates
interviewing from another timezone get at least one round in their own
morning. Every candidate for the same role sees the same loop structure,
and interviewers score against the same rubric so scores are comparable.
Sales roles include a role-play round in place of the peer interview.

## Candidate Experience

Candidates receive the schedule at least three business days in advance,
with the names and titles of their interviewers. Interviewers are expected
to start on time, and late starts are reported to the recruiter. The
decision is made within five business days of the final round, and the
recruiter communicates the outcome to the candidate within that window,
whether it is an offer or a rejection. Rejected candidates can request
feedback once, and the recruiter provides written feedback within ten
business days. Candidates who decline an offer are asked the reason in a
short survey; declination reasons are reviewed quarterly by HR for
patterns.

## Offer Approval

Offers are approved against the salary bands in the Salary Bands document
before they are extended. Authority thresholds: offers up to $120,000 are
approved by the hiring manager and the HR manager; offers from $120,000 to
$150,000 need the department director; offers above $150,000 need the
department VP. Offers outside the band for the level are not extended: the
recruiter either re-scopes the level or escalates to the CHRO and the CFO
for an exception, which is granted only in writing and reviewed at the
six-month mark. Sign-on bonuses are limited to 10% of base salary without
VP approval. Offers are valid for five business days, and start dates are
set for the first Monday of a month unless the candidate and manager agree
otherwise.

## Employee Referral Bonus

Employees can refer candidates through the referral portal. The referral
bonus is $1,000 per successful hire, paid in the first paycheck after the
new hire completes 90 days of service. The bonus applies to regular
full-time roles in any department; it does not apply to referrals that
would report to the referrer, and it does not apply to intern conversions.
The $1,000 referral bonus is separate from the customer referral program
run by sales, which pays $2,000 per new customer. Referral bonuses are
taxed as ordinary income. Recruiting does not accept unsolicited resumes
from external agencies without a signed agency agreement; resumes from
non-contracted agencies are treated as referrals from the sender and do
not create a fee obligation.

## Background Checks and Offers

Offers are contingent on a background check, which HR runs after the
candidate accepts and which typically completes within five business
days. The check covers identity, employment history for the past seven
years, education, and criminal record screening where permitted locally.
The offer is rescinded if the check reveals a material discrepancy;
candidates get one chance to explain a discrepancy before a decision.
References are checked for final-round candidates only, with the
candidate's consent. Visa sponsorship is available for senior roles (L5
and above) and is decided by the department VP at the offer stage.

## Onboarding Handover

Once the background check clears, HR creates the onboarding record and
hands off to the hiring manager and operations: IT provisions equipment
through the Equipment Return Policy's provisioning list, facilities
assigns a desk, and the customer onboarding team is notified if the new
hire is in a customer-facing role. The new hire's first day includes the
handbook orientation, security training, and a 30-minute welcome with
their manager. The 90-day check-in is the first formal feedback point,
ahead of the annual review cycle. Any deviation from this handover (for
example, equipment not ready on day one) is reported to the operations
manager by the end of the first week.
