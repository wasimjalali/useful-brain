# Anti-Harassment Policy

## Policy Statement

Northwind Systems is committed to a workplace free of harassment and
discrimination. This policy applies to all employees, contractors,
interns, and visitors, and to all interactions related to work, including
remote work, company social events, and business travel. Harassment based
on any protected characteristic is prohibited, as are retaliation against
anyone who reports harassment or participates in an investigation. The
policy is owned by HR and the CHRO, Lena Fischer, is the final decision
maker on any investigation outcome. This policy does not replace local
law; where local law gives more protection, local law applies.

## What Constitutes Harassment

Harassment is unwelcome conduct that is severe or pervasive enough to
create a hostile work environment, or that results in an adverse
employment decision. Examples include: offensive jokes or slurs,
unwanted physical contact, sexual advances or requests for favors,
bullying or intimidation, demeaning or degrading comments, and sharing
offensive material. A single incident can be harassment if it is severe
enough. This policy also covers harassment that happens digitally: email,
chat, video calls, and social media, including private channels, because
workplace conduct rules follow the work relationship. Jokes between
friends do not stop being reportable conduct, and the standard is how the
conduct is experienced, not how it was intended.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness harassment, report it to your manager, any
member of the HR team, or the CHRO directly. You do not need to report to
your own manager first; you can go straight to HR or the CHRO. Reports
can be made verbally or in writing, and there is no deadline, but early
reporting makes investigation easier. Reports are logged with a code in
the format HR-####, and the reporter receives an acknowledgment within
two business days. Anonymous reports are accepted through the HR portal
and are investigated, though the outcome may be limited by what can be
established without a complainant. Managers who receive a report must
forward it to HR within one business day; they must not investigate it
themselves.

## Investigation Process

HR assigns an investigator, typically the HR manager, within three
business days of the report. The investigation starts within five
business days and is completed within ten business days of the report,
with a written outcome. Both the complainant and the subject are heard,
and witnesses are interviewed where relevant. Investigations are
confidential to the extent possible: information is shared on a
need-to-know basis, and all involved are reminded that discussing the
investigation is a violation of this policy. The investigator can
recommend interim measures while the investigation runs, including
separating the parties or adjusting reporting lines, without prejudice to
the outcome. If the investigator is the subject or has a conflict, the
CHRO appoints a different investigator.

## Outcomes and Discipline

If the investigation finds harassment occurred, discipline ranges from a
written warning to termination, depending on severity and history, and is
decided by HR with the department head. Termination for harassment follows
the Involuntary Termination section of the Termination and Offboarding
Policy (department head approval, HR manager involvement). If the
investigation does not find harassment, the matter is closed and both
parties are informed in writing; a complainant who disagrees can appeal
to the CHRO within ten business days. False reports made in bad faith are
themselves a disciplinary matter, but a report that is simply not
substantiated is not treated as false.

## Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who reports harassment, assists in an
investigation, or refuses to participate in harassing conduct is
prohibited and is itself grounds for discipline up to and including
termination. Retaliation includes: negative performance reviews following
a report, exclusion from opportunities, demotion, and hostile treatment
by the subject or their allies. Managers who retaliate are subject to the
same discipline. If you believe you are being retaliated against, report
it immediately; retaliation reports are investigated under the same
process and timeline as the original complaint. The Performance Review
Policy's calibration process is the safeguard that catches retaliatory
rating changes, and HR reviews the ratings of complainants for 12 months
after a substantiated report.

## Training and Awareness

All employees complete harassment prevention training within 30 days of
hire and annually thereafter, through the training portal. Managers and
directors complete an additional manager-level module covering their
reporting obligations under this policy. Training completion is tracked
by HR, and the annual compliance calendar includes a training completion
check each January. Contractors and interns complete the same core
training. Questions about what constitutes harassment, or about this
policy, go to hr@northwind.example at any time, and answers are
confidential.
