# Leave and Time Off Policy

## Overview and Eligibility

All regular full-time and part-time employees of Northwind Systems are
eligible for paid time off under this policy, regardless of office location.
Eligibility starts on the first day of employment, and entitlement accrues
pro rata for part-time schedules (anything below 0.5 FTE accrues at half the
standard rate). Contractors, interns on fixed-term assignments shorter than
six months, and temporary staff are not covered by this policy; their time
off is governed by their individual agreements. This policy is administered
by the HR team, and questions about balances should go to
hr@northwind.example. Leave requests are tracked with request codes in the
format LOA-####, which appear on pay statements and in the HR portal.

## Annual Leave Entitlements

Annual leave entitlement depends on the office you are employed in. The
baseline calendar is the same everywhere: leave is tracked in days, not
hours, and a full day is eight hours.

| Office | Annual leave days | Public holidays |
|---|---|---|
| Austin (US) | 20 | 11 |
| London (UK) | 25 | 9 |
| Singapore | 18 | 11 |

Leave must be taken within the calendar year it is earned. New hires receive
their full entitlement for the year of hire, prorated from the first day of
the month following their start date. Employees who join after September 30
receive the following year's entitlement immediately and are not expected to
take leave in their first quarter unless they wish to. Unused leave does not
convert to cash except where local law requires otherwise, in which case the
local HR lead applies the statutory minimum.

## Carryover and Expiry

You may carry over unused annual leave into the next calendar year, but the
carryover is capped at five days. Carried days must be used by March 31 of
the year they are carried into; any carried days still unused on April 1 are
forfeited automatically. There is no exception to the March 31 deadline, and
managers cannot approve a second carryover. If you have a scheduled leave
approved before January 1 that extends past March 31, the portion after
March 31 must be drawn from your current-year entitlement, not from carried
days. For London-based employees, statutory leave that cannot be carried
under this cap is handled by the UK HR lead in line with local law, but the
five-day cap is the Northwind default for everyone.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive ten paid sick days per calendar year, renewed on January 1.
Sick days do not roll over. For absences of three consecutive days or more,
a doctor's note or equivalent medical certificate is required and must be
uploaded to the HR portal within five calendar days of your return. Short
absences (one to two days) only need notification to your manager before
9:00 AM local time. If you exhaust your ten sick days, further absences are
unpaid unless covered by short-term disability arrangements, which the HR
team can explain. Sick leave does not affect your annual leave balance, and
managers may not ask for a diagnosis, only for the certificate.

## Bereavement and Other Leave

Employees receive five paid bereavement days per bereavement event for the
death of an immediate family member (spouse or partner, parent, child,
sibling, grandparent, or grandchild). Additional unpaid leave can be
arranged through your manager and the HR team. Northwind also provides paid
time off for jury duty, voting in national elections, and military reserve
training (up to ten days per year for reservists). Time off for medical
appointments does not count against sick leave if the appointment is
scheduled outside your working hours; if it must fall within working hours,
it counts as sick leave unless your manager agrees otherwise in writing.

## Sabbatical

After seven complete years of continuous service, employees may take a
four-week paid sabbatical, in addition to their annual leave. Sabbaticals
must be requested at least three months in advance and are subject to
manager and HR director approval. Only one person from the same team may be
on sabbatical at a time, so popular periods are allocated by tenure. A
sabbatical does not reset your anniversary date for the next sabbatical:
the clock keeps running from your original start date. Taking a sabbatical
is optional; there is no cash alternative.

## Requesting Time Off

Request leave through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for
single days, and four weeks in advance for leave of five days or more.
Your manager approves or rejects the request within five business days.
Approvals follow the manager's discretion with three constraints: no more
than 60% of a team may be on leave on the same day, no leave may be taken
during a customer go-live week without the customer success lead's sign-off,
and leave cannot be scheduled in a way that skips a mandatory compliance
training window (see the regulatory compliance calendar). Rejected requests
can be appealed to the department head, whose decision is final. Approved
leave shows in the HR portal as LOA-#### codes.

## Relationship to Other Policies

Parental leave is a separate entitlement and does not reduce your annual
leave days: see the Parental Leave Policy for the 16-week paid entitlement
and its approval process. Leave taken under this policy runs concurrently
with any statutory family or medical leave you are entitled to locally. The
Return to Work expectations after any leave of more than four weeks are
described in the Remote Work and Flexible Hours Policy. If you are leaving
Northwind, any approved leave after your notice period start date is subject
to the Termination and Offboarding Policy, and unused entitlement is paid
out only where local law requires it.
