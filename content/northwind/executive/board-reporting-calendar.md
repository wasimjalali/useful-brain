# Board Reporting Calendar

## Purpose and Confidentiality

This calendar defines the reporting rhythm between Northwind Systems
management and the board of directors. It is a private document: it
is visible only to the executive department, and board materials
are not distributed outside the executive team. The calendar is
owned by the CEO's office, and the General Counsel, Amelia Brooks,
administers the meeting logistics. The board of directors of
Northwind Systems meets quarterly, and the meetings are scheduled
for the second Tuesday of February, May, August, and November, in
Austin or by video. The December board meeting is added for budget
ratification, per the Budget Planning and Forecasting Policy.

## Meeting Schedule

The quarterly meetings fall on the second Tuesday of February, May,
August, and November. The December meeting (budget ratification)
is scheduled on the second Tuesday of December, and it is a
shorter session focused on the budget and the annual plan. Board
materials are due five business days before each meeting: the
packet includes the financials, the metrics, the pipeline, the
incident summary, and the action items from the prior meeting.
The packet is prepared by the CFO's office (financials), the CEO's
office (metrics and strategy), and legal (governance items).
Materials are distributed through the board portal, and printed
copies are provided only on request.

## Committee Schedule

The board has three standing committees. The audit committee meets
in April and October, aligned with the SOC 2 audit fieldwork per
the Regulatory Compliance Calendar, and reviews the audit evidence,
the financial controls, and the CISO's security metrics. The
compensation committee meets in June, ahead of the compensation
review, and reviews the executive compensation and the severance
policy (see the Executive Severance Policy). The governance
committee meets in January, and reviews board composition,
succession, and the annual director self-assessment. Committee
materials are due five business days before each committee
meeting, and committee minutes are included in the next board
packet.

## Board Compensation

Non-employee directors receive an annual retainer of $50,000, paid
quarterly, plus $2,500 per board meeting attended and $1,500 per
committee meeting attended. The chair of the audit committee
receives an additional $10,000 per year, and the chairs of the
compensation and governance committees receive an additional
$5,000 each. Directors do not receive equity grants beyond the
initial grant at appointment, which is set at the annual meeting.
Director compensation is reviewed by the governance committee in
January, and changes take effect at the annual meeting. Director
expenses follow the Travel Expense Policy and the Employee Expense
Policy, and are reimbursed within 10 business days of approval,
like employee expenses.

## Annual Meeting and Governance

The annual shareholder meeting is held in May, and proxy materials
are finalized 40 days before the meeting date. The annual report
draft goes to the board at the February meeting, covering the
December 31 year-end per the Regulatory Compliance Calendar's
annual filing deadlines. Board elections and director
appointments are effective at the annual meeting. The board
approves: the annual budget (December), the executive severance
policy (December), the M&A thresholds in the Merger and
Acquisition Approval Policy (as deals arise), and the appointment
and removal of officers. Officer appointments are proposed by the
CEO and approved by the board.

## Reporting to the Board

The CEO reports to the board at every meeting: revenue, pipeline,
headcount, product milestones, and the risk register. The CFO
reports the financials, the cash position, and the budget
variance. The CISO reports the security metrics at the May
meeting, per the Security and Vulnerability Policy, and at the
audit committee meetings. The CTO reports the engineering
metrics and the incident summary (SEV-1 and SEV-2 incidents since
the prior meeting). ESC-4 complaints are reported at the next
meeting per the Customer Complaint Escalation Path, and disputes
above $100,000 are reported per the Dispute Resolution and
Arbitration Policy. The board packet template is maintained by
the CEO's office.

## Records and Confidentiality

Board materials, minutes, and committee records are retained for
ten years, which is longer than the standard retention in the
Data Retention Policy because board records are corporate
records; the General Counsel is the custodian. Board materials
are confidential under the IP and Confidentiality Policy and are
not shared outside the executive department. Directors sign the
director NDA under the NDA Policy at appointment. The board
calendar is reviewed annually in December, and changes to the
schedule are announced at least 30 days in advance except for
emergency meetings, which can be called by the CEO or the board
chair with 48 hours of notice.
