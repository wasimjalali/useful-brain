# Intellectual Property and Confidentiality Policy

## What This Policy Covers

This policy covers two related obligations for everyone who works with
Northwind Systems: intellectual property (IP) ownership and
confidentiality. It applies to employees, contractors, interns, and
board members, and it survives employment: the confidentiality and
non-solicit obligations continue after departure. The policy is owned
by legal, and questions go to legal@northwind.example. The NDA Policy
covers agreements with outside parties; this policy covers the internal
obligations of people who work here. The Privacy Policy covers customer
data specifically; this policy covers everything else that is
confidential.

## Ownership of Work Product

Anything you create in the course of your work for Northwind belongs to
Northwind: code, designs, documents, customer materials, internal tools,
and inventions. This includes work created on personal devices and work
created outside normal hours if it relates to your role or uses company
resources. The employee handbook's orientation includes the invention
assignment acknowledgement, which all employees sign at hire and again
at each promotion to L5 and above. Contractors assign IP under their
agreements, and the Contractor Expense Policy's sponsor is responsible
for confirming the assignment clause is signed before work starts. If
you are unsure whether a project belongs to Northwind, ask legal before
sharing it anywhere.

## Confidential Information

Confidential information includes: customer data, pricing and discount
terms, financial results, salary and band data, product roadmaps,
security details, and anything marked confidential. You may use
confidential information only to do your job, and you may share it only
with people who need it for their job. Confidential information is not
discussed in public spaces (elevators, cafes, transit), on personal
devices without the security controls in the Security and Vulnerability
Policy, or with family and friends. Customer data gets extra care: it is
handled only in approved systems, never copied to personal accounts, and
never used for anything other than serving the customer, per the
Privacy Policy.

## Post-Employment Obligations

Confidentiality obligations continue after employment ends. In
addition, employees at manager level and above sign a 12-month
non-solicit clause: for 12 months after departure, they may not solicit
Northwind employees to leave, and may not solicit Northwind customers
for competing products where they had direct account responsibility.
The non-solicit clause does not restrict general job postings or
responding to a customer who initiates contact. Departing employees
return all confidential material with their equipment under the
Equipment Return Policy, and their obligations are restated in the exit
paperwork under the Termination and Offboarding Policy. Legal can waive
the non-solicit in writing for a specific departure, and the waiver is
the exception, not the rule.

## Handling Breaches

A suspected breach of this policy is reported to security at
security@northwind.example or to legal. Security investigates technical
leaks (see the Incident Escalation Matrix for SEV-1 through SEV-4
severity), and legal investigates non-technical leaks. Breaches are
assessed for: what was exposed, whether it was customer data (which
triggers the customer notification rules in the Privacy Policy), and
whether it was intentional. Consequences range from a written warning to
termination for cause under the Termination and Offboarding Policy, and
legal may pursue remedies for intentional breaches. Employees who report
a breach in good faith are not penalized for the report itself.

## Open Source and External Materials

Using open source software at work is governed by the Software
Development Lifecycle policy's license review step; every dependency
is checked for license compatibility before it enters the codebase.
Contributing to open source projects on company time requires manager
approval, and contributions must not include company confidential
information. Using company materials (templates, code samples, designs)
for personal projects is not allowed without written permission from the
department head. Publishing anything about Northwind, including blog
posts and conference talks about your work, requires a review by
communications in the executive office, which completes within five
business days.

## Relationship to Other Policies

The NDA Policy covers confidentiality agreements with third parties
before we share anything with them. The Data Retention Policy controls
how long confidential records are kept, and this policy does not change
any retention period. The Security and Vulnerability Policy covers the
technical controls that protect confidential information, and the
Business Continuity Policy covers what happens to confidential data in
an emergency. The Anti-Harassment Policy is separate: this policy is
about information, not conduct. If a conflict appears between this
policy and another, legal decides which applies.
