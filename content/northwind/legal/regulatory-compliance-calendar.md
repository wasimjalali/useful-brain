# Regulatory Compliance Calendar

## Purpose

This calendar lists the fixed compliance and regulatory deadlines
Northwind Systems must meet every year. It is owned by legal and is the
reference for finance (filing and audit deadlines), HR (training and
record deadlines), and the executive team (board reporting). Each entry
lists the deadline, the owner, and the control it satisfies. The
calendar year is the fiscal year, and deadlines are firm: the teams
named here plan their work around them. The calendar is reviewed and
reissued by legal each December for the following year.

## Annual Filings

March 31: annual corporate report filing for all three entities
(Austin, London, Singapore), owned by finance with legal review. April
30: UK year-end payroll reporting (PAYE final submission) for the tax
year ending April 5, owned by payroll. July 31: US year-end
information returns and recipient copies (forms issued to contractors
and employees), owned by payroll, following the Tax Withholding
Policy. October 31: Singapore CPF year-end reconciliation, owned by
payroll. December 1: insurance program renewal (cyber, D&O, and
general liability), owned by finance with the COO. December 31:
fiscal year end, and the annual report draft goes to the board for the
February board meeting, per the Board Reporting Calendar.

## Audits and Certifications

April and October: SOC 2 Type II audits, covering the security and
privacy controls in the Security and Vulnerability Policy and the
Privacy Policy; the auditor's fieldwork runs the first two weeks of
April and October, and the reports are delivered 60 days after
fieldwork. June: annual privacy review, owned by legal with the CISO,
covering the subprocessor list, DSAR metrics, breach log, and privacy
training completion. ISO 27001 recertification runs every three years,
with surveillance audits in the two years between; the current
certificate expires in March 2027, so the recertification audit runs
January 2027. Quarterly: finance's budget-vs-actuals reports (published
by the 10th of the following month), feeding the audit evidence.

## Tax Deadlines

US payroll tax deposits follow the statutory schedule, and the
quarterly payroll tax returns are due on the last day of the month
following each quarter (April 30, July 31, October 31, January 31).
UK VAT returns are quarterly, due one calendar month and seven days
after the quarter end, and are handled by finance with the London
office. Singapore GST returns are quarterly, due by the last day of
the month following the quarter. Contractor payments above $600 in a
calendar year are reported by January 31 (US), per the Tax Withholding
Policy. The finance team reconciles tax accounts monthly, and the
annual corporate tax returns are filed by the statutory deadlines in
each country, with the March 31 filing as the anchor for entity
filings.

## Training Deadlines

January: annual harassment prevention training completion check, per
the Anti-Harassment Policy; all employees must complete the annual
module by January 31. New hires complete it within 30 days of hire.
March: annual security awareness training, tied to the March band
review month, completed by March 31. The Regulatory Compliance Calendar
does not schedule the training content; it schedules the completion
deadlines and the reporting to the CHRO and CISO. Training completion
is tracked by HR, and a completion rate below 95% at the deadline is
escalated to the CHRO and the CISO.

## Legal and Board Deadlines

The board meets quarterly on the second Tuesday of February, May,
August, and November, and the budget ratification is added to the
December board meeting per the Budget Planning and Forecasting Policy.
Board materials are due five business days before each meeting, per
the Board Reporting Calendar. The audit committee meets in April and
October, aligned with the SOC 2 fieldwork. The compensation committee
meets in June, ahead of the compensation review window. The annual
shareholder meeting is held in May, and the proxy materials are
finalized 40 days before. Legal owns the meeting calendar and sends
the deadline reminders 30 days, 14 days, and 7 days out.

## Escalation and Missed Deadlines

A missed deadline is reported to the General Counsel the same day it is
identified, with the reason and a remediation plan. Missed regulatory
filings are additionally reported to the CFO and the CEO, because they
can carry penalties and affect the audit opinion. Remediation plans
include a root cause note and a control change, and the General Counsel
tracks remediation to completion. Two missed deadlines in one year in
the same control area trigger a process review of that area. This
calendar is distributed to legal, finance, HR, and the executive team
in December, and changes mid-year are announced by legal with at least
30 days of notice where possible.
