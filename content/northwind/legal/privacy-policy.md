# Privacy Policy

## What This Policy Covers

This policy explains how Northwind Systems handles personal data: data
about our customers' end users, about our customers' employees, and
about our own employees, prospects, and website visitors. It is owned
by legal, with the CISO, Priya Raman, responsible for the technical
controls. The policy applies in all three offices and to every product:
Northwind Core, Atlas, and Meridian. The Data Retention Policy covers
retention periods generally; this policy covers personal data rights
and processing rules specifically. Questions go to privacy@northwind.example,
and customer data requests are handled through the Customer Data Access
Requests process.

## Personal Data We Process

Northwind processes personal data in four categories: customer account
data (names, work emails, billing contacts), customer end-user data
(data our customers' users store in our products, which we process on
the customer's behalf), employee data (the HR records covered by the
Data Retention Policy), and prospect data (marketing contacts, covered
by the 24-month prospect retention rule). We process personal data only
for the purpose it was collected: to deliver the products, to bill, to
support, and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data,
and we do not use customer end-user data for our own advertising or
product training without written consent.

## Customer Data Processing

For customer end-user data, Northwind is a processor acting on the
customer's instructions under the data processing addendum (DPA) in
every customer contract (see the Contract Review and Approval Policy).
We process end-user data only to provide the contracted services, and
we do not access it except for support cases with the customer's
authorization, security incidents under the Incident Escalation Matrix,
or legal process. Subprocessors are listed in the DPA and the customer
is notified 30 days before a new subprocessor is added, with an
objection window of 30 days. When a contract ends, end-user data is
deleted 90 days later unless the customer requests earlier deletion,
which is completed within 30 days of the request.

## Individual Rights

Individuals whose personal data Northwind holds have the right to
access, correct, export, and delete their data, and to object to
processing. Requests are made through the Customer Data Access Requests
process (DSAR-#### codes) or directly to privacy@northwind.example.
Northwind responds within 30 days; where a request is complex or the
volume is large, the response can be extended by up to 60 days, with
notice to the requester. Deletion requests are completed within 30
days. Identity is verified before any request is fulfilled: for
customer end-user data, the request goes through the customer's
administrator, and for employee data, through HR. Refused requests are
answered with the legal basis for refusal.

## Security and Breach Notification

Personal data is protected by the controls in the Security and
Vulnerability Policy: encryption in transit and at rest, access
controls, and the incident response process. A breach involving
personal data is assessed under the Incident Escalation Matrix; a
breach that creates a risk to individuals is reported to the affected
customer within 72 hours of confirmation, and to the relevant
supervisory authority where local law requires (the UK GDPR regime
applies to London operations). The CISO decides whether a breach is
reportable, and legal reviews the notification. Breach notifications
include what happened, what data was involved, and what we are doing
about it. Notification templates are kept current by legal.

## Marketing and Website Data

Prospect data is collected through website forms, events, and content
downloads, and is used for sales outreach under the 24-month prospect
retention rule in the Data Retention Policy. Marketing emails include
an unsubscribe link, and opt-outs are processed within 10 business days
and kept permanently. Website analytics use aggregated data; where
analytics identify individuals, the same 24-month rule applies.
Employees' personal data is processed for employment purposes per the
Employee Handbook and the HR policies, and employee data requests go
through HR under the same 30-day response window.

## Governance and Audits

Privacy governance sits with legal and the CISO. The annual privacy
review runs in June, per the Regulatory Compliance Calendar, and covers:
the subprocessor list, the breach log, DSAR metrics, and training
completion. Privacy training is part of onboarding and annual security
training. The April and October SOC 2 audits include the privacy
controls. The CISO reports privacy metrics to the board at the May
board meeting. Violations of this policy are handled under the IP and
Confidentiality Policy's breach process, and intentional violations are
grounds for termination under the Termination and Offboarding Policy.
