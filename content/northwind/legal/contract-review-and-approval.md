# Contract Review and Approval

## When Legal Review Is Required

Northwind Systems uses standard contract templates for most business.
The templates cover: the Northwind master services agreement (MSA) with
the standard terms, the data processing addendum (DPA), and the partner
agreement. Legal review is required for any contract that deviates from
a template, and the review thresholds are based on annual contract
value (ACV). Contracts below $100,000 ACV use the standard template
without legal review; the sales team can sign them under the proposal
process. Contracts from $100,000 to $250,000 ACV require legal review
before signature. Contracts from $250,000 to $1,000,000 require legal
review plus approval from the CFO and the General Counsel. Contracts
above $1,000,000 require legal review plus approval from the CFO, the
General Counsel, and the CEO.

## Standard Terms

The standard MSA has fixed terms that sales cannot change: payment is
Net 30 from invoice, the initial term is 12 months with automatic
annual renewal, liability is capped at 12 months of fees paid, and
termination for convenience requires 90 days written notice. Discounts
are not part of the MSA; they are set in the order form under the
Discount Approval Policy. Refunds are governed by the Refund Policy and
are not written into individual contracts. The standard DPA incorporates
the Privacy Policy's data handling rules, including the 30-day deletion
response and the 90-day post-contract retention. Any change to these
standard terms is a deviation and triggers legal review regardless of
the ACV.

## Approval Authority

Approval authority for contracts: the sales VP approves contracts up to
$100,000. The CFO approves contracts from $100,000 to $250,000 (after
legal review). The CFO and General Counsel jointly approve contracts
from $250,000 to $1,000,000. Above $1,000,000, the CEO joins the CFO
and General Counsel. Approvals are recorded in the contract system with
the contract code (CT-####), and the system blocks signature until the
required approvals are present. The approval chain is separate from the
discount approval chain: a contract can have an approved discount but
still need contract-level approval, and vice versa. Approvers cannot
approve contracts they are a party to, and the CEO's own employment
contract is approved by the board under the Severance Policy rules.

## Redlines and Turnaround

Legal reviews redlines and returns contracts within five business days
for contracts under $250,000, and within ten business days for contracts
at or above $250,000. The sales team can request expedited review
(24 hours) for competitive situations, with the General Counsel's
approval, and expedited requests are limited to two per quarter per
salesperson. Redlines are tracked in the contract system, and both
sides see the version history. A contract that goes through more than
two rounds of redlines is escalated to the General Counsel. Standard
template contracts do not get redlined; they are signed as-is.

## Contract Lifecycle

Contracts are signed in the electronic signature system, and the
executed copy is stored with its CT-#### code in the contract system.
The contract system tracks renewals: automatic renewals are flagged 60
days before the renewal date, and the account owner confirms the renewal
under the Proposal and Quote Process. Amendments are contracts
themselves and follow the same review thresholds based on the amended
ACV. Contract files are retained for seven years after the contract
ends, per the Data Retention Policy, then archived for three more
years. The finance team reconciles signed contracts to invoices monthly,
and an unsigned contract with active invoicing is a billing error under
the Invoicing and Payment Terms Policy.

## Related Policies

The Discount Approval Policy governs discount percentages and is applied
at the order form stage, before contract signature. The NDA Policy
governs confidentiality agreements, which are signed before contract
discussions begin. The Dispute Resolution and Arbitration Policy governs
what happens when a contract dispute reaches the dispute stage; the
standard MSA incorporates its 60-day negotiation and binding
arbitration clauses. The Merger and Acquisition Approval Policy governs
deals involving other companies, which are not contracts under this
policy. Contract questions from customers go through support, and legal
answers within the turnaround times above.
